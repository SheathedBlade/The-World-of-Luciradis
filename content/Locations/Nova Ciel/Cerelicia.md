## Geography and Environment

Situated along the coast of [[Luciradis#Nova Ciel|Nova Ciel]], Cerelicia is characterized by its maritime setting and the close relationship between its urban districts and the surrounding natural environment. The city's architectural traditions make extensive use of open terraces, shaded public spaces, and cultivated vegetation, reflecting the importance of its coastal surroundings to everyday life.

The region's native ecosystems include coastal vegetation and marine communities adapted to local climatic conditions and variations in [[Ambient Arcana|ambient arcana]]. Certain species exhibit physiological responses to changes in ambient concentration, contributing to seasonal differences in flowering, biological activity, and other observable natural phenomena.

Although urban development has altered portions of the original environment, cultivated gardens and remaining coastal habitats continue to support native organisms. The interaction between these habitats and the city's expanding infrastructure remains an area of interest within [[Arcane Ecology|arcane ecology]].
