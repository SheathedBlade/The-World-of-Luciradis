Nova Ciel refers to the group of landmasses situated in the southern hemisphere of Luciradis. Its name, roughly translated from ancient languages as _“New Sky,”_ follows the naming tradition associated with Cicatri Ciel, in which the skies above the land reflect the experiences and aspirations of its inhabitants.

The region became a destination for those fleeing the devastation of the Scarring. Among its early settlers were migrants from Cicatri Ciel who sought refuge from the conflicts that had consumed their homelands. Their hopes of establishing new lives beyond the war-torn northern lands would come to be reflected in the name Nova Ciel, symbolizing a new beginning for those who settled there.
