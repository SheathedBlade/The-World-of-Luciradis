## Etymology

Derived from the tongues of the [[Luciradis#Nova Ciel|southern landmass]], Gensōkyo is roughly translated to the "Land of Illusions". Such is aptly named when the offshoot clan of nomad akumaros settled in the dense forests of western Cicatri Ciel.
