Cicatri Ciel refers to the group of landmasses situated in the northern hemisphere of Luciradis. Its name, roughly translated from ancient languages as _“Scarred Sky,”_ originated in the aftermath of the catastrophic conflict known as the Scarring.

During this conflict, a war between beings of extraordinary power fractured what had once been a continuous landmass, leaving behind the divided geography that characterizes the region today. The name Cicatri Ciel has endured as a reminder of this devastation and its lasting influence upon the region's history.
