The **Fundamentals** collection encompasses the foundational knowledge necessary to understand Luciradis, its history, and the natural principles governing its existence. Drawing upon historical scholarship and contemporary scientific research, these articles establish the concepts upon which much of the world's civilizations, technologies, and understanding of arcana are built.

The subjects documented herein range from the world's geographical and historical origins to the nature of arcana, its environmental presence, and the enduring influence of the Aurelants.

## Principal Subjects

**[[Luciradis]].** An overview of the world, its geographical divisions, natural characteristics, and historical development, including the events that shaped the civilizations of Cicatri Ciel and Nova Ciel.

**[[The Aurelants]].** An examination of the twelve beings who inherited the Authorities following the defeat of Genesis, their subsequent influence upon the world, and the nature of alluvia and Ascendance.

**[[Astos Calendar]].** The principal system of timekeeping employed throughout Luciradis, encompassing its historical origins, chronological designations, twelve Aurelant months, and conventions for recording dates.

**[[Principles of Arcana]].** The fundamental principles governing arcana, its deliberate manipulation by practitioners, and the relationship between individual aptitude, alluvia, and the application of arcane techniques.

**[[Ambient Arcana]].** The naturally occurring presence of arcana throughout Luciradis, including its environmental distribution, interactions with the natural world, and phenomena associated with fluctuations, depletion, and suppression.
