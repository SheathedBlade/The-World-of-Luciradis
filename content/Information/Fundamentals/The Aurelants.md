> [!archive] Historical Record
> *Originally published by the Congregation of Academia, Luxor Citadel*  
> *Revised and annotated by Oracle's Vision*

**The Aurelants** are twelve legendary figures whose actions have profoundly shaped the history of Luciradis. Each is associated with a distinct Authority, granting powers far beyond those ordinarily attainable through arcana. Although the precise circumstances surrounding their rise to power remain a subject of historical inquiry, the influence of the Aurelants persists through the essences dispersed during **the Sundering**.

These essences reside within living beings and influence an individual's natural affinity for arcana associated with their corresponding Authorities. Their concentrations vary considerably, with those possessing the greatest concentration of a particular essence recognized as Ascendants. Such individuals frequently demonstrate capabilities that distinguish them from even the most accomplished practitioners.

The relationship between the Aurelants, their Authorities, and the broader practice of arcana remains a principal subject of scholarly research. Over successive generations, scholars have sought to understand the distribution of these essences, the abilities they confer, and the circumstances through which their influence has endured.

Despite considerable advances in this field, much about the Aurelants remains uncertain. While the properties of their surviving essences can be observed and measured, the full extent of their Authorities and the events that shaped their history have yet to be conclusively established.

## Origins and the Sundering

The origins of the Aurelants can be traced to the Era of Genesis, when twelve mortals embarked upon a journey that would ultimately culminate in the death of Genesis, the being traditionally regarded as the creator of existence. Though their individual backgrounds and motivations differed, they were united in their opposition to the reverence afforded to Genesis and the authority it held over the world.

Following Genesis's defeat, the twelve came into possession of extraordinary powers that would become known as Authorities. Each Authority granted its bearer influence over a distinct aspect of existence, elevating the former mortals to a position of power unprecedented in recorded history. Their subsequent actions would shape the course of civilization and establish the foundations of their enduring legacy.

Their unity, however, did not endure. Disagreements among the Aurelants eventually escalated into a prolonged conflict remembered as **[[The Scarring|the Scarring]]**, during which their extraordinary powers brought devastation across the world. The conflict was ultimately brought to an end through the intervention of **[[The Aurelants#The Creator|the Creator]]**, one of the twelve Aurelants, who enacted **the Sundering**.

Through the Sundering, the Authorities of the other eleven Aurelants were divided into countless essences and dispersed throughout the world. The Creator alone remained unaffected, retaining their Authority. These dispersed essences would become the foundation of the relationship between the original Aurelants and the Ascendants who emerged in subsequent generations.

Although the broad sequence of these events is well established, surviving accounts differ in their interpretations of the Aurelants' intentions, the causes of the Scarring, and the circumstances surrounding its conclusion. Consequently, the events of this period remain subjects of continued historical examination.

## Essence

Essences are remnants of the Authorities dispersed during the Sundering, now present throughout living populations. Although their precise nature remains incompletely understood, their influence upon an individual's aptitude for arcana has been extensively documented. Each essence retains an association with its corresponding Authority, allowing its influence to be distinguished from that of others.

An individual may possess multiple essences in varying concentrations. These concentrations influence their natural aptitude for arcana associated with the corresponding Authorities, with greater abundance generally allowing for more advanced or demanding manifestations. The distribution of essence differs considerably between individuals, contributing to the variation observed in their natural capabilities.

While essence plays a significant role in determining aptitude, it does not independently confer mastery. The practice of arcana requires knowledge, training, and experience, and individuals with comparatively modest concentrations may nevertheless develop considerable proficiency through dedicated study. Conversely, a strong natural affinity does not guarantee effective application without the necessary understanding and discipline.

Arcana itself is not dependent upon the existence of the Aurelants or their essences. Its practice predates their rise to power, and the influence of essence is better understood as a factor affecting an individual's capabilities rather than the origin of arcana. The precise relationship between essence concentration and the full range of abilities attainable through arcana remains an active subject of research.

## Ascendance

**Ascendant** is the title bestowed upon individuals recognized as possessing the greatest concentrations of an essence associated with a particular Aurelant. Such individuals commonly demonstrate exceptional aptitude for arcana related to their corresponding Authority, often exhibiting capabilities far beyond those of ordinary practitioners.

The title of Ascendant is not permanent, nor does it represent a distinct state of existence. Should another individual surpass the current holder in the concentration of the corresponding essence, they may be recognized as the succeeding Ascendant. This transfer of title is commonly referred to as **dethronement** and does not, in itself, alter the abilities of either individual.

Although essence concentration is the principal criterion for Ascendance, it does not independently determine an individual's capabilities. Knowledge, training, experience, and personal aptitude influence how an individual's affinity manifests. Consequently, successive Ascendants associated with the same Authority may demonstrate substantially different abilities, despite sharing a common source of essence.

The capabilities exhibited by an Ascendant should not be mistaken for the full extent of the original Aurelant's Authority. While their exceptional concentrations of essence may permit extraordinary applications of arcana, they remain distinct from the powers once possessed by the original bearers.

## Identification and Measurement

The identification of Ascendants is made possible through specialized thaumaturgical apparatuses capable of detecting and measuring the presence and concentration of essences within an individual.

Such instruments are commonly found in medical facilities throughout [[Locations/Cicatri Ciel/index|Cicatri Ciel]], where they serve as an established means of assessing natural affinities for arcana and identifying individuals whose essence concentrations may qualify them for recognition as Ascendants.

The measurements obtained through these apparatuses allow physicians and scholars to distinguish between essences associated with different Authorities and evaluate their relative abundance within a subject. These findings provide an important basis for the documentation and recognition of Ascendants, as well as the maintenance of historical and contemporary records.

Beyond their role in identifying exceptional individuals, essence measurements contribute to broader investigations into magical aptitude. Comparative observations allow scholars to examine variations in essence concentration and their relationship to the manifestations of arcana, providing valuable information for both theoretical and applied research.

The continued refinement of these instruments has substantially improved the precision and reliability of essence measurement. Nevertheless, their findings remain limited to the properties they are designed to observe. An assessment of essence concentration cannot, by itself, establish the full extent of an individual's knowledge, technical mastery, or capacity to manifest extraordinary abilities.

## Manifestations of Arcana

The influence of essence upon arcana is not expressed uniformly among individuals. Although each essence is associated with a particular Authority, the abilities developed through that affinity may differ considerably in their form, application, and complexity.

These variations arise from the interaction between an individual's natural aptitude, accumulated knowledge, and personal understanding of arcana. Two practitioners possessing an affinity for the same Authority may therefore develop entirely different techniques, even when their essence concentrations are comparable.

For instance, an individual possessing an affinity for [[The Aurelants#The Radiant|the Radiant]] may specialize in the manipulation of light, while another may develop techniques involving heat or other forms of radiative energy. Both applications arise from the same underlying affinity, yet differ according to the practitioner's knowledge and chosen methods.

Such diversity is particularly evident among Ascendants, whose exceptional essence concentrations may permit applications beyond those ordinarily attainable through arcana. Nevertheless, their abilities remain subject to individual interpretation and do not necessarily reproduce the techniques or capabilities of the original Aurelants.

The study of these manifestations remains essential to understanding the relationship between essence and arcana. Rather than defining a fixed collection of abilities, an individual's affinity establishes a range of possibilities whose practical expression depends upon their capacity to understand and apply them.

## Social Significance

The influence of the Aurelants extends beyond the study of arcana, having shaped religious traditions, political institutions, and cultural identities throughout the history of Luciradis. Their role in the defeat of Genesis and the subsequent events of the Scarring has inspired widely differing interpretations, ranging from reverence for their extraordinary achievements to condemnation of the devastation associated with their legacy.

Ascendants, as individuals recognized for possessing exceptional concentrations of essence, occupy a similarly complex position within society. Their standing varies considerably between cultures and individuals, with some attaining positions of considerable influence while others remain relatively removed from public affairs. Although their capabilities may inspire admiration, suspicion, or fear, the title of Ascendant carries no inherent political or social authority.

The significance attributed to particular Authorities also varies between cultures and historical periods. Some traditions associate them with ideals or virtues, while others emphasize the dangers posed by the powers they represent. These interpretations have contributed to the development of distinct religious practices, historical narratives, and social expectations surrounding individuals associated with their essences.

In the modern era, continued advances in the study of essence and arcana have encouraged a more systematic understanding of these phenomena. Nevertheless, longstanding cultural beliefs remain influential, and the relationship between Ascendants and the societies in which they live continues to reflect both the historical legacy of the Aurelants and contemporary attitudes toward extraordinary power.

## The Twelve Aurelants

Each of the twelve Aurelants came to possess a distinct Authority following the defeat of Genesis. These Authorities encompass extraordinary powers associated with different aspects of existence, and their surviving essences continue to influence the practice of arcana throughout Luciradis.

The following entries provide an overview of each Authority, its known properties, and the abilities associated with its essence. While their general characteristics have been documented through historical accounts and the study of subsequent Ascendants, the full extent of the original Authorities remains incompletely understood.

### The Creator

**Known Ascendants:** None documented

Much of what is known about the Creator originates from fragmented historical accounts and surviving legends of the Scarring. Among the twelve Aurelants, the Creator is distinguished by an extraordinary command over the formation and reconstitution of matter through the manipulation of spiritual energy, having ultimately prevailed over the other eleven during the conflict.

Deciphered texts describe the Creator's ability to convert surrounding spiritual energy into physical manifestations, allowing the formation of objects and structures that had not previously existed. This capability also extended to the destruction and reconstitution of existing matter, although the precise mechanisms underlying these transformations remain incompletely understood.

According to surviving accounts, the immense loss of life during the Scarring contributed to an increasing abundance of spiritual energy, which the Creator was able to harness to devastating effect. As the conflict continued, this growing availability of energy afforded the Creator a considerable advantage, eventually allowing them to overcome the other Aurelants.

The final surviving accounts describe the conclusion of the Scarring and the subsequent disappearance of the Creator. Through an extraordinary act remembered as the Sundering, the Authorities of the other eleven Aurelants were divided and dispersed throughout the world. The Creator alone remained whole, retaining their Authority. No reliable historical records have since established the whereabouts of the original bearer, and no Ascendant associated with the Creator has been documented.

Of particular interest is a possible connection between the Creator and the [[Factions of Luciradis#Luxori Covenant|Luxori Covenant]], a prominent religious institution whose earliest surviving texts date to approximately the same period as the Creator's disappearance. The Covenant's reverence for the Creator and the teachings attributed to them have prompted speculation regarding the institution's origins. However, attempts to substantiate this relationship have yielded little beyond conflicting accounts and religious traditions, leaving the matter unresolved.

### The Expanse

**Known Ascendants:** [[Factions of Luciradis#Brood of Azharel|Orsilus of Azharel]] (former); [[Saria Pulse]] (current)

The Expanse's Authority encompasses **primal expression**, governing the instincts, physical capabilities, and natural affinities of living beings. Its influence is most readily observed through heightened sensory perception, extraordinary physical prowess, and an instinctive connection with surrounding life.

Historical accounts of the Scarring describe the original bearer as a dragon of immense proportions, possessing brilliant white scales and wings of a vibrant amethyst hue. Among the abilities attributed to the Expanse was an extraordinary awareness of nearby living creatures, extending to the perception of even the faintest heartbeat. The bearer was also said to possess an exceptional affinity with beasts of land, sea, and sky, rallying them during the conflicts that followed the downfall of Genesis.

The Expanse has maintained a longstanding association with the [[Factions of Luciradis#Brood of Azharel|Brood of Azharel]], an ancient draconic lineage whose traditions center upon the preservation of its essence. Unlike the succession of many other Ascendants, the inheritance of the Expanse within this brood is governed by an established rite, through which its essence is transferred from one bearer to a designated successor.

Prospective successors are expected to undergo a trial before participating in the ritual, a practice intended to ensure that the title of Ascendant remains within the lineage. Although the precise mechanisms of this rite remain closely guarded by the brood, its continued observance has enabled successive generations to preserve the title within their lineage.

Despite its historical association with dragons, the Expanse is not inherently restricted to those of purely draconic ancestry. The recognition of Saria Pulse, an individual of [[Inhabitants of Luciradis#Vyrian|mixed human and draconic heritage]], as its current Ascendant demonstrates that its essence may be inherited and manifested by those of mixed lineage. Its manifestations encompass heightened instincts, physical enhancement, and the strengthening of natural affinities with other living beings, although their precise expression varies between individuals.

### The Paragon

**Known Ascendants:** [[Leoris Varens]] (former); [[Arzen Lynos]] (current) 

The Paragon's Authority encompasses **physical mastery** and fortitude, granting extraordinary strength, endurance, resilience, and resistance to destructive forces. Its manifestations extend beyond individual physical enhancement, including defensive capabilities that allow practitioners to withstand and protect against otherwise overwhelming forces.

Historical accounts of the Scarring describe the original bearer as a formidable protector whose strength and resilience were matched by an exceptional proficiency in arcana. The Paragon was said to have defended a region that would eventually become part of the [[Stalwryn Kingdom|Kingdom of Stalwryn]], safeguarding its inhabitants against the growing devastation of the conflict.

This commitment to protection, however, eventually gave way to retaliation. Following an attack by the Seer upon a nearby village, the Paragon turned against the other Aurelants in pursuit of vengeance. Although initially motivated by the defense of those under their protection, the ensuing campaign brought considerable destruction to the surrounding lands. Surviving accounts describe a figure whose determination increasingly overshadowed the very responsibilities that had first compelled them to fight.

In subsequent generations, the Paragon became particularly associated with defensive arcana, owing in part to the prominence of its Ascendants within the military traditions of Stalwryn. While these manifestations frequently emphasize exceptional durability, protective techniques, and the capacity to withstand otherwise overwhelming forces, they represent only a portion of the Authority's broader capabilities.

The precise expression of the Paragon varies between individuals. Some Ascendants have demonstrated exceptional defensive proficiency, while others have exhibited extraordinary physical performance and resilience. Such differences illustrate the versatility of physical mastery and the influence of an individual's training, experience, and personal approach upon the manifestation of their essence.

### The Seer

**Known Ascendants:** [[Iris Riedel]] (current)

The Seer's Authority encompasses **transcendent perception**, allowing its bearer to perceive information beyond the limitations of ordinary senses. Its most widely documented manifestation is foresight, through which individuals may glimpse events that have yet to occur. The clarity, scope, and duration of these visions vary considerably between practitioners, ranging from moments into the immediate future to events that may unfold decades or even centuries later.

The practical applications of foresight are similarly diverse. Some practitioners develop an exceptional awareness of imminent events, allowing them to anticipate an opponent's movements and respond with remarkable precision. Others experience visions of more distant possibilities, although such revelations may lack the context necessary for accurate interpretation. Consequently, even remarkably detailed predictions cannot always be regarded as definitive accounts of events yet to unfold.

Beyond foresight, historical accounts attribute extraordinary perceptive capabilities to the original Seer, including an unusual sensitivity to the soul and spiritual energy. The precise nature of these abilities remains poorly understood, and surviving descriptions offer insufficient evidence to determine whether they constituted a distinct application of perception or involved other properties of the Authority.

Records preceding the Scarring describe a marked deterioration in the original Seer's relationship with the other Aurelants. Although earlier accounts suggest that their association had once been considerably more amicable, later texts speak of growing resentment and hostility. This change is frequently associated with a revelation witnessed through the Seer's foresight, purportedly depicting the eventual downfall of the twelve.

Whether this vision directly influenced the events of the Scarring, or merely reflected circumstances already developing among the Aurelants, remains a matter of historical debate. Nevertheless, the account has endured as one of the most frequently cited examples of the uncertainties inherent in interpreting visions of the future.

### The Horizon

**Known Ascendants:** [[Amara Illustros|Unknown]] 

The Horizon's Authority encompasses **celestial and otherworldly phenomena**, permitting extraordinary interactions with forces and influences originating beyond the familiar terrestrial environment. Its manifestations are commonly associated with celestial bodies, the forces governing their movements, and phenomena whose origins lie beyond the observable world.

Among the twelve Aurelants, the Horizon is frequently described as one of the least inclined toward conflict. Surviving accounts of the Era of Genesis portray an individual who spent considerable periods observing the heavens, seemingly more interested in the mysteries of the cosmos than in the affairs of those around them. The reasons for this fascination remain unknown, although the behavior has become one of the most recognizable characteristics attributed to the original bearer.

During the Scarring, the Horizon reportedly took little interest in the hostilities between the Aurelants, intervening primarily when directly threatened. Consequently, historical accounts provide relatively few descriptions of their abilities in combat. Those that survive suggest an exceptional command over celestial forces, though the precise nature and limitations of these manifestations remain uncertain.

Ancient folklore also attributes unusual forms of psychokinesis and telepathy to the Horizon. These accounts describe the movement of objects without physical contact and the communication of thoughts between individuals. Whether such abilities represent applications of the Horizon's Authority or reflect later interpretations of poorly understood phenomena remains a subject of scholarly debate.

Records concerning successive Ascendants of the Horizon remain exceptionally scarce, with the identity of its current bearer absent from publicly available registries. This scarcity has limited systematic observation of its manifestations, leaving much of the Authority's potential understood primarily through historical accounts and theoretical research.

### The Lifeline

**Known Ascendants:** [[Karos Varens]] (last known)

The Lifeline's Authority encompasses **continuance**, governing the preservation, restoration, and sustenance of living beings. Its manifestations are most commonly associated with healing arcana, through which practitioners may accelerate recovery, mend injuries, and preserve vital functions that would otherwise deteriorate.

Historical accounts describe the original Lifeline as an exceptionally accomplished practitioner of restorative arcana, whose abilities extended far beyond those attainable through conventional healing techniques. Although references to the original bearer appear in numerous surviving texts, relatively few provide detailed descriptions of their activities during the Scarring.

While healing remains its most widely recognized application, the Lifeline's influence is not limited to the restoration of injuries. More advanced manifestations may involve the manipulation of vitality itself, including the preservation, redistribution, or depletion of the forces that sustain living beings. Such applications demonstrate that the Authority's influence over life is not necessarily confined to restorative purposes.

Despite these capabilities, the extent to which the Lifeline's Authority can overcome the natural limitations of living organisms remains uncertain. Its association with continuance should not be interpreted as an unconditional capacity to reverse death or preserve life indefinitely, as such claims have yet to be substantiated through reliable historical or contemporary observations.

### The Reflection

**Known Ascendants:** [[Erina Hayashi]] (current)

The Reflection's Authority encompasses **experienced reality**, governing how individuals perceive, interpret, and experience the world around them. Its manifestations are most commonly associated with illusory arcana, through which practitioners may alter sensory perceptions, influence cognition, and construct experiences that differ considerably from their physical surroundings.

Historical accounts identify the original Reflection as an [[Inhabitants of Luciradis#Akumari|akumari]] originating from the southern landmass of [[Locations/Nova Ciel/index|Nova Ciel]]. According to surviving traditions, the original bearer belonged to a nomadic community whose members eventually divided over their future. Those who remained in the south became known as the [[Factions of Luciradis#Dreamweavers (Yumori-te)|Dreamweavers]], or Yumori-te, while those who accompanied the Reflection northward to Cicatri Ciel came to be recognized as [[Factions of Luciradis#Illusionists (Gensōsenshi)|the Illusionists]], or Gensōsenshi.

The Illusionists eventually established the settlement of [[Gensōkyo]] within the dense forests of western Cicatri Ciel. Its name, derived from the language of their ancestral homeland, translates approximately to "Land of Illusions." Although separated by geography, the two factions retained a shared cultural heritage, developing distinct traditions and approaches to arcana over successive generations.

Surviving accounts describe the original Reflection as a restless individual whose curiosity and desire to explore seldom diminished. Despite having helped guide the Illusionists to their new homeland, the original bearer eventually departed once more, joining a company of travelers whose journey would later become inseparable from the history of the Aurelants.

The Reflection's influence remains particularly evident in the traditions of the Illusionists and Dreamweavers. While their techniques differ in approach and application, both demonstrate how the manipulation of perceived experience can produce effects extending well beyond the simple creation of visual illusions. The distinction between altering an individual's experience and changing the physical world itself remains central to understanding the nature of the Reflection's Authority.

### The Navigator

**Known Ascendants:** [[Cyrus Orico]] (former); [[Rietta Aschlera]] (current)

The Navigator's Authority encompasses **elemental flow**, governing the movement, interaction, and regulation of natural elements. Its manifestations are most commonly associated with elemental arcana, allowing practitioners to manipulate water, air, earth, fire, and other natural phenomena through a variety of techniques.

The earliest surviving accounts of the Navigator originate from sailors who spoke of an extraordinary individual capable of guiding vessels through otherwise treacherous waters. According to these accounts, the original bearer possessed an exceptional understanding of the natural environment, navigating unfamiliar seas with remarkable precision while mitigating the dangers posed by unfavorable weather and ocean currents. It was through these exploits that the individual became known as the Navigator.

Beyond these maritime accounts, historical texts attribute considerable influence over the natural environment to the original bearer. The Navigator is frequently credited with contributing to the prosperity of [[Galaderyn]], where favorable conditions encouraged abundant harvests and the flourishing of local wildlife. During the Scarring, however, these same capabilities were employed to devastating effect, with surviving accounts describing violent storms and other natural disasters brought about through the manipulation of elemental forces.

The destruction associated with these events is often cited as a contributing factor in the increasing isolation of Galaderyn's elven communities. Over successive generations, this separation reinforced the development of distinct cultural traditions, particularly those concerned with the study and application of elemental arcana.

The Navigator's essence has maintained a particularly strong association with elven society, with documented Ascendants predominantly belonging to elven lineages. Within the [[Factions of Luciradis#Ferros Republic|Ferros Republic]], individuals recognized as Ascendants of the Navigator are accorded the status of sovereign elves, irrespective of their previous standing. This distinction reflects the considerable importance attributed to elemental proficiency and the enduring influence of the Navigator upon elven society.

Although its manifestations frequently involve mastery over a particular element, the Navigator's Authority is not confined to any single elemental discipline. Successive Ascendants may develop markedly different specializations, with some demonstrating exceptional command over water or atmospheric phenomena while others excel in different applications of elemental arcana. Such variations reflect the breadth of elemental flow and the influence of individual aptitude, training, and experience.

### The Resonance

**Known Ascendants:** Unknown

The Resonance's Authority encompasses **harmonic correspondence**, governing the relationships through which distinct forces, energies, and phenomena may influence one another. Its manifestations are commonly associated with the manipulation of vibrations, frequencies, and resonance, allowing practitioners to amplify, diminish, or otherwise influence existing phenomena through their interactions.

Although the Resonance is recognized among the twelve Aurelants, relatively little is known about its original bearer. Surviving historical accounts provide few reliable descriptions of their actions during the Scarring, and the circumstances surrounding their involvement in the conflict remain largely undocumented.

The Resonance's essence is understood to permit applications extending beyond the manipulation of sound alone. Through the understanding and regulation of compatible frequencies or patterns, practitioners may influence the behavior of physical materials, transmit disturbances across a medium, or alter the intensity of existing phenomena. Although its influence upon vibrations and harmonic interactions is well understood, the extent to which these principles may be applied to more complex phenomena remains an active subject of scholarly investigation.

### The Radiant

**Known Ascendants:** [[Runo Illustros]] (former); [[Varant Illustros]] & [[Altair Illustros]] (current)

The Radiant's Authority encompasses **radiative energy**, governing the emission, transmission, and absorption of energy through radiation. Its manifestations are most commonly associated with the manipulation of light and heat, although its influence extends to forms of radiation beyond those ordinarily perceptible to the senses.

Practitioners possessing an affinity for the Radiant may develop techniques involving the generation and direction of illumination, the concentration of radiant heat, or the suppression of light within a particular area. More advanced applications may involve the manipulation of otherwise invisible radiation, allowing for effects that differ considerably from conventional light-based arcana.

Historically, the Radiant has maintained a prominent association with **Luxor**, where its manifestations have influenced the development of arcana and the cultural significance attributed to light. Although the Authority is commonly understood through its more visible applications, these represent only a portion of the phenomena encompassed by radiative energy.

The Radiant's manifestations may differ considerably between individuals. While some practitioners specialize in illumination or the projection of concentrated energy, others may pursue techniques involving thermal radiation or the regulation of energy within their surroundings. Such differences reflect the breadth of the Authority's domain and the influence of individual knowledge and specialization upon its practical expression.

### The Axiom

**Known Ascendants:** [[Kallie Myrai]] (current)

The Axiom's Authority encompasses **knowledge and comprehension**, granting an extraordinary capacity to acquire, retain, and understand information. Its manifestations extend beyond conventional memory, allowing individuals to recognize patterns, establish connections between seemingly unrelated observations, and comprehend subjects of remarkable complexity.

Among the most distinctive abilities attributed to the original bearer was the preservation of knowledge within the soul itself. Historical accounts describe an individual capable of retaining an extensive record of their experiences, including information that might otherwise have been lost through the passage of time. This extraordinary accumulation of knowledge has contributed greatly to the Axiom's longstanding association with scholarship and the preservation of history.

Despite the intellectual capabilities associated with the Authority, surviving accounts portray the original Axiom as a curious and relatively inexperienced individual. The bearer belonged to an early [[Inhabitants of Luciradis#Terrakin|terrakin]] community whose understanding of the wider world remained limited. Dissatisfied with the confines of their settlement, the young terrakin sought to explore lands beyond those familiar to their people, eventually joining the company of travelers who would become the twelve Aurelants.

The distinction between the original bearer's modest beginnings and their later abilities remains an important part of the Axiom's historical legacy. Their pursuit of knowledge preceded the acquisition of an Authority, suggesting that the curiosity for which they became renowned was a characteristic of the individual rather than a consequence of their extraordinary powers.

The Axiom's essence continues to influence the study, preservation, and interpretation of knowledge. Although its most recognizable manifestations concern memory and comprehension, the extent to which such abilities may permit the recovery, reconstruction, or interpretation of otherwise inaccessible information remains an active subject of scholarly investigation.

### The Convergence

**Known Ascendants:** Unknown

The Convergence's Authority encompasses **synthesis and transformation**, allowing the combination, refinement, and reconfiguration of existing materials and their properties. Its manifestations are most commonly associated with alchemy and craftsmanship, through which practitioners may alter the composition and characteristics of substances to produce materials and objects with otherwise unattainable qualities.

Historical accounts identify the original bearer as a master artisan, widely credited with the development and popularization of the sundial. Through this invention, the artisan introduced a practical means of measuring the passage of time using the movement of shadows cast by the sun. The resulting demand for their services led to the establishment of a workshop, where numerous apprentices were trained in the principles of craftsmanship and the study of materials.

Despite the success of these endeavors, the artisan eventually departed to travel alongside a company of adventurers, leaving behind an extensive collection of blueprints, observations, and technical records. These materials would later form the foundations of a tradition of craftsmanship that endured long after the original bearer's departure.

During the Scarring, the Convergence became renowned for the creation of extraordinary weapons and artifacts, including firearms, blades, and other implements whose properties exceeded those attainable through conventional methods. Several of these relics survive in historical collections, although the techniques employed in their construction have largely been lost. The ability to extract, combine, and refine the properties of existing materials nevertheless remains among the most recognizable manifestations of the Convergence's essence.

In the generations that followed the artisan's departure, their former apprentices carried these teachings throughout Cicatri Ciel. Their descendants and successors gradually established new centers of craftsmanship, adapting the original methods to emerging technologies while preserving portions of the knowledge entrusted to them.

Although the Convergence is primarily associated with material transformation, the full extent of its Authority remains incompletely understood. Its applications demonstrate that synthesis need not be limited to the combination of physical substances, and that the properties of a resulting creation may differ considerably from those of its constituent materials.

## Scholarly Research

The study of the Aurelants, their Authorities, and the essences dispersed during the Sundering has remained a significant field of scholarly inquiry throughout the Era of Ascendance and subsequent historical periods.

During the Era of Ascendance, the **Congregation of Academia** contributed substantially to the classification and documentation of these phenomena. Its investigations established important foundations for understanding essence concentration, variations in natural aptitude for arcana, and the identification of Ascendants. The historical records and theoretical frameworks developed during this period continue to inform contemporary scholarship.

The continued refinement of these principles has been supported by the **[[Luciradis Authority of Magical Sciences]] (LAMS)**, whose research, regulatory standards, and documentation practices have contributed to a more systematic understanding of essence and arcana.

Within LAMS, investigations into the fundamental properties of essence and its relationship with arcana are complemented by the work of its **Division of Applied Thaumaturgy & Physics**, which develops instruments and practical methods for observing and measuring these phenomena. The **Central Registry** maintains records of documented findings and recognized Ascendants, contributing to the preservation and accessibility of scholarly information.

Despite these advances, the existence of sophisticated measurement techniques and extensive historical records should not be mistaken for a complete understanding of the Aurelants or their Authorities. Questions concerning the precise nature of their powers, the mechanisms of the Sundering, and the full range of manifestations associated with their essences remain subjects of continued investigation.

As scholarship advances, established interpretations may require reconsideration in light of newly documented phenomena and previously inaccessible historical evidence. The continued examination of these findings remains essential to understanding the enduring influence of the Aurelants upon Luciradis.
