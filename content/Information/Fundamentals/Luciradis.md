> **Geography, History & Natural Sciences**

Luciradis is the world inhabited by numerous civilizations, cultures, and forms of life. Its geography, natural environments, and history have been profoundly shaped by arcana, a force woven into the foundations of the world itself.

The world is broadly divided into two major geographical regions: **[[Locations/Cicatri Ciel/index|Cicatri Ciel]]**, situated predominantly within the northern hemisphere, and **[[Locations/Nova Ciel/index|Nova Ciel]]**, situated within the southern hemisphere. Though each possesses distinct environments and histories, both share a common origin and have been shaped by the events that followed the Scarring.

## Etymology

The name **Luciradis** roughly translates to _"shining root"_ in ancient tongues. Its origins are traditionally associated with the creation of the world, although the precise linguistic and historical circumstances surrounding its adoption remain uncertain.

The association between light and roots has persisted throughout the world's recorded history, reflecting longstanding interpretations of Luciradis as a world sustained by forces extending beyond its visible natural environment.

## Geography

Luciradis encompasses a diverse range of geographical environments, including extensive forests, mountain ranges, valleys, plains, and bodies of water. The distribution of these environments is influenced by both conventional natural processes and the presence of **arcana**.

Certain regions exhibit unusually high concentrations of [[ambient arcana]], resulting in environments whose characteristics may differ considerably from those found elsewhere. Such regions can support distinctive ecosystems and forms of life that have adapted to their surroundings over successive generations.

### Cicatri Ciel

**Cicatri Ciel** refers to the group of landmasses situated predominantly within the northern hemisphere of Luciradis. Widely regarded as the cradle of civilization, the region has served as the setting for many of the world's most consequential historical events, from the earliest known civilizations to the conflicts and transformations that shaped the modern era.

Prior to the Scarring, much of the region formed a largely continuous landmass known as **Asterra**. Throughout the Era of Genesis, Asterra was home to numerous independent civilizations, each possessing its own cultures, traditions, and political institutions.

The Scarring irrevocably altered its geography, fracturing the landmass and separating territories that had once been connected. In the aftermath, the collective designation _Cicatri Ciel_, roughly translated as _"Scarred Sky"_ in ancient tongues, gradually emerged to describe the fractured northern lands.

Although Cicatri Ciel eventually became the predominant geographical designation, the name Asterra remains preserved in historical accounts of the world before the Scarring.

### Nova Ciel

**Nova Ciel** encompasses the principal group of landmasses situated within the southern hemisphere of Luciradis.

Unlike Cicatri Ciel, its modern name was adopted by emigrants seeking refuge from the devastation and instability that followed the Scarring. This migration, historically associated with the **[[Events/Cicatri Emigration|Cicatri Emigration]]**, contributed to the establishment of new settlements throughout the southern lands.

The name _Nova Ciel_, roughly translated as _"New Sky"_, deliberately follows the naming convention of its northern counterpart, symbolizing the aspirations of those seeking to establish new lives beyond their ancestral homelands.

The region had no universally recognized name among the arriving emigrants prior to their settlement.

Over time, the establishment of permanent settlements contributed to the development of new communities and cultural identities, increasingly distinct from those of Cicatri Ciel.

## History

The history of Luciradis is traditionally divided into several major eras, distinguished by events that profoundly altered the development of civilizations and their relationship with the [[The Aurelants|Aurelants]].

### Era of Genesis

The **Era of Genesis** represents the earliest recognized period in the history of Luciradis, preceding the rise of the Aurelants and the devastation of the Scarring.

During this era, numerous independent civilizations flourished across Asterra and beyond. The period is most notably associated with the original party of twelve mortals who opposed Genesis, the being traditionally regarded as the creator of existence.

Their eventual victory over Genesis and inheritance of the Authorities marked a turning point in the world's history, giving rise to the original Aurelants.

### The Scarring

The **[[Events/The Scarring|Scarring]]** was a catastrophic conflict involving the Aurelants that fundamentally altered the geography of Luciradis.

Among its most significant consequences was the fragmentation of Asterra, giving rise to the divided northern lands now known as Cicatri Ciel. The conflict may also have contributed to lasting disturbances in the natural environment and the distribution of arcana throughout affected regions.

The conclusion of the Scarring subsequently became the foundation of the modern chronological system.

### Era of Ascendance

The **Era of Ascendance** followed the Scarring, marking a period of reconstruction, political transformation, and the development of new civilizations and institutions.

The influence of the Aurelants and the emergence of successive [[The Aurelants#Ascendance|Ascendants]] played an important role in shaping the societies of this era. Many institutions, traditions, and political structures originating during Ascendance would continue to influence the world for generations.

### Era of Freedom

The **Era of Freedom** succeeded the Era of Ascendance, marking a further transformation in the political and social order of Luciradis.

Although many institutions and traditions from preceding eras endured, their roles evolved alongside the changing circumstances of the world. The contemporary scholarly activities of Oracle's Vision, including the compilation and preservation of historical records, belong to this era.

## Arcana and the Natural World

Arcana is an intrinsic aspect of Luciradis, influencing both the physical environment and the capabilities of its inhabitants.

Its manifestations extend beyond deliberate magical practice. Arcana occurs naturally throughout the world, and its presence may influence the development, behavior, and survival of living organisms.

The systematic study of these phenomena falls within the discipline of **arcanics**.

### The Roots

Conventional magical practices draw upon fundamental foundations of the world known as **roots**.

Among the recognized roots are:
- **Root of Earth**
- **Root of Water**
- **Root of Fire**
- **Root of Wind**

These roots provide a foundation for understanding conventional magical phenomena. Their relationship to the Authorities of the Aurelants and the alluvia distributed following the Sundering remains a subject of further scholarly consideration.

### Arcane Ecology

The natural environments of Luciradis are shaped by the interaction of ordinary ecological processes and ambient arcana.

Over successive generations, living organisms may develop adaptations suited to environments with particular arcane characteristics. The extent to which different species depend upon arcana varies considerably; some exhibit limited interaction with it, while others possess physiological or behavioral adaptations that allow them to draw upon surrounding concentrations naturally.

Such adaptations are especially apparent in regions where arcana is unusually abundant, although the presence of arcana alone does not necessarily determine an ecosystem's composition.

Further information concerning these adaptations and the relationship between arcana and living organisms may be found in [[Arcane Ecology]].

## System of Time

The **[[Astos Calendar]]** is the principal system of timekeeping employed throughout Luciradis. Named in honor of [[Astos Yerazel]], the original Aurelant of the Convergence, the calendar was standardized following the Scarring and has remained in use throughout subsequent historical eras.

The calendar comprises twelve months of thirty days each, named after the [[The Aurelants|Aurelants]] in their established order. An additional period known as **Renewal** concludes the year, lasting five days in ordinary years and six in leap years. The beginning of the year is traditionally aligned with the spring equinox of the northern hemisphere.

Years are numbered relative to the conclusion of the Scarring, using **BSC** (_Before Scarring_) and **ASC** (_After Scarring_). These designations remain consistent across historical periods, including the Era of Ascendance and Era of Freedom.

Contemporary dates ordinarily use a shortened notation, such as **Horizon's 16th, 10110 ASC (16/05/10110)**. Further information concerning the calendar's history, structure, and conventions may be found in [[Astos Calendar]].
