**The Empyrean Concepts** are twelve metaphysical manifestations associated with the distinct principles of arcana, whose influence has profoundly shaped the development of civilization throughout Luciradis. Although their precise origins remain the subject of historical and theological inquiry, their existence is most readily observed through the presence of **Empyrean essences**, remnants of their respective powers that reside now within living beings.

These essences influence an individual's natural affinity for particular disciplines of arcana, granting varying degrees of proficiency in the magical principles associated with each Concept. Those possessing exceptionally high concentrations of a particular essence are recognized as **Ascendants**, individuals whose capabilities frequently distinguish them from even the most accomplished practitioners.

The relationship between the Concepts, their essences, and the broader practice of arcana constitutes one of the principal subjects of arcanics. Over successive generations, scholars have sought to understand the nature of these metaphysical entities, the distribution of their powers, and the mechanisms through which their influence spreads among mortals. 

Despite considerable advances in this field, the Empyrean Concepts remain incompletely understood. Their influence is readily measurable, yet the circumstances of their emergence and the full extent of their properties continue to challenge established scholarship.

## Origins of the Empyrean Concepts

The origins of the Empyrean Concepts are traditionally associated with the events of **[[The Scarring|the Scarring]]**, a devastating conflict whose consequences fundamentally altered the relationship between mortals and arcana.

Surviving historical accounts offer differing interpretations of the Concepts' existence before this conflict. Some portray them as primordial embodiments of the principles they govern, while others suggest that their present forms emerged through transformations associated with the Scarring itself. The scarcity of reliable contemporary records have prevented a definitive consensus.

What is more widely accepted is that the Scarring culminated in a phenomenon known as **[[The Scarring#The Sundering|the Sundering]]**, during which the powers of the Concepts were divided and dispersed throughout the world. Although the precise circumstances surrounding this event remain uncertain, its consequences are considered fundamental to the subsequent emergence of Ascendants.

The distributed remnants of the Concepts' powers came to be known as Empyrean essences, establishing a new relationship between mortals and the metaphysical principles once associated with the Concepts themselves.

The Sundering is therefore regarded as one of the most consequential events in the recorded history of arcana. Its aftermath laid the foundations for the system of essence inheritance and Ascendance that would come to characterize the following era.

While the Scarring and the Sundering are frequently distinguished in scholarly discourse, the latter is generally understood as the culminating event of the former rather than an independent historical conflict.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist
> The surviving accounts of the Scarring are considerably less complete than their frequent citation might suggest. Much of our present understanding relies upon interpretations made generations after the conflict had ended.
> 
> In particular, the distinction between the original bearers of the Concepts and the metaphysical entities recognized in subsequent eras warrants greater scrutiny. Certain discrepancies cannot be adequately explained by the prevailing historical account alone.

## Empyrean Essence
Empyrean essence refers to the distributed remnants of power associated with the twelve Empyrean Concepts. These essences exist within living being, influencing their natural aptitude for the disciplines of arcana corresponding to their respective Concepts.

An individual may posses the essences of multiple Concepts simultaneously, though their concentrations are seldom uniform. Consequently, a practitioner mat demonstrate considerable affinity for one magical discipline while exhibiting more modest capabilities in another. This variation is understood to be among the principal factors contributing to the diversity of magical proficiencies observed throughout Luciradis.

The concentration of a particular essence bears a strong relationship to an individual's natural proficiency in the corresponding discipline. Greater concentrations generally confer stronger affinity, allowing practitioners to comprehend and exercise associated forms of arcana with greater ease. Individuals possessing exceptionally dense essences mat exhibit capabilities that surpass those of their peers, even when their formal instruction or practical experience is comparatively limited.

However, essence concentration should not be regarded as an absolute measure of magical accomplishment.

The effective practice of arcana requires knowledge, experience, discipline, and an understanding of the principles being manipulated. While an abundance of essence may provide substantial natural advantages, such potential must still be developed and refined through practice. A scholar who has dedicated decades to the study of a particular discipline may consequently possess greater technical understanding than an individual whose natural affinity is considerably stronger.

Furthermore, individuals possessing similar concentrations of the same essence may develop markedly different specializations. Personal inclinations, methods of instruction, and circumstances of practice all contribute to the forms through which their abilities are expressed.

This distinction is particularly important when examining the relationship between Empyrean essence and the broader practice of arcana. Although the Concepts exert a profound influence upon magical aptitude, arcana itself is not understood to have originated exclusively from their existence. Its practice encompasses principles and disciplines extending beyond the inheritance of Empyrean power.

As such, the possession of a particular essence neither guarantees mastery of every associated technique nor necessarily prevents the study of disciplines outside one's strongest affinities.

## Ascendance

The designation of **Ascendant** refers to an individual possessing the greatest concentration of essence associated with a particular Empyrean Concept.

Such individuals are distinguished by an extraordinary natural affinity for their corresponding disciplines of arcana, frequently demonstrating capabilities that exceed those of conventional practitioners. Throughout the Era of Ascendance, the recognition of these individuals became an important subject of both magical scholarship and public interest.

Unlike hereditary titles or offices granted through political appointment, Ascendance is determined by the relative concentration of Empyrean essence.

The designation is therefore not necessarily permanent. Should another individual emerge with a greater concentration of the corresponding essence, the incumbent may be supplanted by a new Ascendant. This succession does not inherently require the defeat, death, of willing abdication of the previous bearer, as the determining principle is the concentration of essence itself.

Although Ascendants ordinarily exhibit remarkable proficiency in the disciplines associated with their Concepts, their abilities are not necessarily identical. Differences in specialization, aptitude, and personal experience may result in considerable variation between successive bearers of the same Concept.

An Ascendant may excel in one expression of their inherited power while possessing comparatively little understanding of another. Likewise, extraordinary essence concentrations do not inherently grant access to every manifestation theoretically associated with a Concept.

The designation of Ascendant should therefore be understood as a distinction of exceptional Empyrean affinity rather than an assertion of absolute mastery over a magical discipline.

## Identification and Measurement

The identification of Ascendants is made possible through specialized thaumaturgical apparatuses capable of measuring the presence and concentration of Empyrean essences within an individual.

Such instruments are commonly found in medical facilities throughout [[Locations/Cicatri Ciel/index|Cicatri Ciel]], where they serve as an established means of assessing magical affinities and identifying individuals whose essence concentrations may qualify them for Ascendance.

The measurements obtained through these apparatuses allow physicians and scholars to distinguish between the essences associated with different Concepts and to evaluate their relative abundance within a subject. Such findings form an important basis for the documentation and classification of known Ascendants.

Beyond their role in identifying exceptional individuals, essence measurements contribute to broader investigations into magical aptitude. Comparative observations allow scholars to study variations in essence concentration and their relationship to the manifestations of arcana, providing valuable information for both theoretical and applied research.

The reliability of these instruments has made them indispensable to the study of Empyrean phenomena. Nevertheless, their measurements remain limited to the properties they are designed to observe.

As assessment of essence concentration cannot, by itself, establish the full extent of an individual's knowledge, technical mastery, or capacity to manifest extraordinary magical phenomena.

## Manifestations of Arcana

Although each Empyrean Concept is associated with a recognizable metaphysical principle, the manifestations of its corresponding arcana may differ considerably between individuals.

Such variations are not necessarily indicative of separate sources of power. Rather, they may arise from differing interpretations and applications of the same underlying principle, influenced by an individual's aptitude, specialization, and understanding.

The [[Empyrean Concepts#The Seer|Seer]], for instance, is principally associated with foresight. Among practitioners exhibiting an affinity for this Concept, the ability to perceive events beyond the immediate present may manifest in markedly different forms.

An individual experienced in combat may develop the capacity to anticipate an opponent's movements moments before they occur, allowing them to respond with exceptional precision. Another practitioner may instead experience visions of distant events, perceiving possibilities whose realization lies far beyond the immediate future.

Both manifestations arise from the same general principle, yet their practical applications differ substantially.

Similarly, the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Reflection|Reflection]] is associated with the manipulation of cognition and perception. The traditions of the [[Factions of Luciradis#Illusionists (Gensōsenshi)|Illusionists]] and [[Factions of Luciradis#Dreamweavers (Yumori-te)|Dreamweavers]] demonstrate distinct applications of this principle, with both possessing an exceptional affinity for arcana concerned with the mind.

While the particular techniques employed by these groups may differ, their shared relationship to the Reflection illustrates how a single Concept can give rise to multiple recognizable disciplines.

The [[Empyrean Concepts#The Convergence|Convergence]] presents another notable example. Its associated arcana has become particularly prominent in craftsmanship, alchemy, and the refinement of materials, where practitioners employ their abilities in the creation and transformation of objects.

Yet historical accounts concerning the original bearer suggest a relationship with the study and measurement of time, indicating that the commonly recognized applications of a Concept may not necessarily encompass the full extent of its underlying principles.

Such differences have led scholars to distinguish between a Concept's broadly recognized magical domain and the individual techniques through which that domain is expressed.

Consequently, the classification of arcana remains a developing field, subject to revision as new manifestations are documented and previously established theories are reconsidered.

While such exceptions don't necessarily invalidate established theory, they serve as a reminder that our classifications should remain open to revision as our understanding develops.
## Social Significance

The recognition of an Ascendant may carry considerable social, cultural, and political significance.

Throughout Cicatri Ciel, exceptional magical aptitude has frequently been regarded as a mark of distinction, particularly in societies where arcana plays a major role in governance, craftsmanship, scholarship, or military service. The identification of an Ascendant may consequently influence an individual's reputation, professional opportunities, or standing within their community.

The extent of such recognition varies considerable between nations and institutions. While some societies attach particular prestige to those bearing concentrated Empyrean essence, others place greater emphasis upon demonstrated ability, scholarly accomplishment, or established social position.

Nevertheless, the prominence of Ascendants within historical accounts reflects the enduring influence of Empyrean essence upon the civilizations of Luciradis.

## The Twelve Empyrean Concepts

Twelve Empyrean concepts are recognized within the established classification of magical 
phenomena. Each is associated with a distinct metaphysical principle, influencing the natural affinities and manifestations of arcana observed among those possessing its essence.

Although their associated disciplines are commonly described in broad terms, these classifications should not be regarded as exhaustive. The manifestations of a particular Concept may extend beyond its most familiar applications, and successive Ascendants have occasionally demonstrated abilities that challenge prevailing interpretations.

The following registry records the recognized Concepts, their associated magical principles, and the individuals identified as Ascendants throughout recorded history.

### The Creator

Not much is known about the Creator, save for a few faded texts and legends. The Creator boasted power unlike any other, and as such triumphed over all other Concepts in **The Scarring**. Its power was as its namesake: the power to create, but deciphered texts suggest the Creator also had a mastery over destruction as well. The contents as to what it creates can only be inferred from the remaining ancient texts, which state that the Creator had wielded immense spiritual power, capable of manifesting objects of power through the conversion of the surrounding spiritual energy. As such, the deaths of many during the Scarring only served to empower the Creator, eventually surpassing the powers of all other Concepts. As they say, destruction and creation are two sides of the same coin.

The last texts that our scholars have translated dealt with the aftermath of the Scarring and the subsequent disappearance of the Creator. While all the other Concepts were reduced to their very essence, the Creator was kept whole. Past that, it is inferred that the Creator had subsequently disappeared from the world, as no new texts succeeding our latest were found about the whereabouts of the Creator. Therefore, combined with these deciphered texts along with our own observations, it is accurate to say that there are no Ascendants housing the essence of the Creator.

Although traces of the Creator end there, there is reason to believe that there is a connection between it and the prevailing religion of Cicatri Ciel: the [[Factions of Luciradis#Luxori Covenant|Luxori Covenant]]. The original texts that founded this religion date not too far back from when the Creator's whereabouts mysteriously vanished. Given that their religion worships the Creator and its "teachings", it may not be a coincidence, but attempts at further research into this line of thinking only led to more fictitious accounts, and we have thus ceased this avenue of inquiry.

### The Expanse

**Previous Ascendant:** [[Factions of Luciradis#Brood of Arodraenix|Orsilus of Arodraenix]]  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Saria Pulse]]

The Expanse is a title referring to the ancient dragon of legend, Arodraenix, during **the Scarring**, and is known for its heightened primal senses. The most notable aspect of this is the ability to keenly hear the beats of the heart. In addition to this, the Expanse is known to effortlessly rally all the beasts of the land, sea, and sky to their side. During the Scarring, this power proved very useful in not only the defense of its territory, but also the conquest of lands beyond its own. It's aptly named as such from the vivid drawings from ancient texts, giving the wings a vibrant amethyst color alongside the blindingly white scales of the dragon.

The descendants of Arodraenix bear the power of the Expanse, giving priority to the [[Factions of Luciradis#Brood of Arodraenix|new breed of dragons]] that emerged unscathed from the Scarring. Therefore, it is highly unlikely, if not impossible, that non-draconic beings would be able to have any essence of the Expanse.

### The Paragon

**Previous Ascendant:** [[Leoris Varens]]  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Arzen Lynos]]

The Paragon is known for their stalwart protection against the Concepts during the Scarring. It doesn't hesitate to retaliate against those who wrong him for not only its sake, but for the sake of the beings it is protecting. Its manifestation of power is skewed towards the protection and retaliation of others, which can range between powerful offensive magicks to extremely resilient defensive magicks. It is not uncommon for Ascendants to be seen using both offensive and defensive magicks, but since the rise of the [[Factions of Luciradis#Monarchy of Stalwryn|Monarchy of Stalwryn]], Paragon Ascendants are mostly seen with powerful defensive magicks.

When **the Scarring** first began, the Paragon was stationed in a certain strip of land that would later become territory of the Stalwryn Kingdom. Possessing the strength and resilience, alongside the most powerful invocations, the Paragon began their attack on the other Concepts once the Seer had attacked a small village in its vicinity. Much to the dismay of the villagers he was protecting, the Paragon paved a path of vengeance, never looking back to see the destruction it had wrought.

### The Seer

**Previous Ascendant:** [[Driardos Malairse]]  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Iris Riedel]]

The Seer is known for their foresight and perception into the future. This ability manifests differently in most Ascendants, giving each Ascendant different time ranges at which the Ascendant can peer into the future. It can range from a few seconds to centuries into the future, the former of which is quite useful in combat since it allows the Ascendant to quickly read their foe's movements. In all instances, the power only grants the user a glimpse, specific enough to detail what might happen, but vague enough to remove any and all context from that moment.

In addition to incredible perception, another trait of the original Seer was their masterful control over the soul and spiritual energy. It is not the same as soul manipulation, however, but it refers to

Deciphered texts preceding **the Scarring** described the Seer holding immense hatred and contempt towards the other Empyrean Concepts before the events of **the Scarring**, though previous texts would suggest otherwise. It is said that it was then that the Seer peered into the future and witnessed a revelation: the future beheld the end of the Empyrean Concepts.

### The Horizon

**Previous Ascendant:** Unknown  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Amara Illustros|Unknown]]

The Horizon, as was written in the ancient texts, was notably the most passive of the Concepts. When threatened, it will act out of self-defense, however this is the only known scenario in which we can discern its feats during the Scarring. Instead, most of the texts name the Horizon aptly for its unending activity of looking skyward. What it thinks about when looking up to the heavens is unknown.

In certain folklores of eld, the Horizon boasted incredible celestial magic, using astrology with the positions of celestial bodies in the sky. Depictions of the Horizon shows that it is capable of a much more primitive version of psychokinesis, which does include telepathy. However, from what we know now of telepathy, it requires a strong bond with the individual it is trying to influence. As such, it can be assumed that its would-be Ascendant would be equally as strong, no matter the amount of remnant within the individual. However, even with its overwhelming power, the Horizon rarely utilized it, according to ancient texts. Even less so, there are very few instances where an Ascendant of this Concept has emerged publicly.

### The Lifeline

**Last Known Ascendant:** [[Karos Varens]]

The Lifeline, as depicted by various ancient texts around the continent, was a master of healing invocations.

### The Reflection

**Previous Ascendant:** Unknown  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Erina Hayashi]]

The Reflection was noted to be a master of illusory arts, capable of distorting an individual's cognition beyond their recognition. The individual behind the title is not native to Cicatri Ciel, but an akumari native from the [[Locations/index#Nova Ciel|southern landmass]]. According to the akumari elders of [[Gensōkyo]], they came with a following of nomadic akumari, looking for a place to settle. Strife had followed the Reflection, and so their nomadic clan split into two; those that followed the Reflection were known as the [[Factions of Luciradis#Illusionists (Gensōsenshi)|Illusionists]], while those who stayed behind dubbed themselves the [[Factions of Luciradis#Dreamweavers (Yumori-te)|Dreamweavers]].

Scrolls from the temples of Gensōkyo depict the Reflection as an individual that never liked to stay in one place. Thus, it was at a certain point in time that the famous akumari departed from the Illusionists' new abode in search of more adventure. They joined a party of adventurers and travelers, and was not seen again for decades until the advent of **the Scarring**.

### The Navigator

**Previous Ascendant:** [[Cyrus Orico]]  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Rietta Aschlera]]

The first records of the Navigator were discovered through the ramblings of sailors, speaking of a person that could only be described as a natural compass. It was able to guide ships to their destinations without fail, and without duels with the unpleasant seas. Thus, this person was given the title of the Navigator.

The Navigator is most known for their masterful control over elements of nature, which include proficiencies in invocations of all [[Luciradis#System of Magic|Roots of Magic]]. In the ancient texts, it is said that the Navigator was directly responsible for the thriving haven of [[Galaderyn]], showering their land with plentiful harvests and rich wildlife. However, during **the Scarring**, the Navigator is known to conjure various natural disasters in the conflict of power. These natural disasters are what led to the isolation of the elves, their race never peeking outside their gates to see the razed land beyond them.

The Navigator's powers all manifest as masteries of separate elements. For example, an Ascendant could be capable of only water-bending, while another could be proficient at manipulating the winds. All records of the Navigator's Ascendants show that each and every one of these Ascendants are of elven descent, as practiced invokers and manawards from the [[Factions of Luciradis#Ferros Republic|Ferros Republic]] share many similarities with the Navigator.

### The Axiom

**Previous Ascendant:** Unknown  
**Current Ascendant:** [[Kallie Myrai]]

The Axiom is a Concept best known for their thirst, and subsequent storage, of knowledge. All that the Axiom interacts with is recorded within their very soul. To that end, all of our current knowledge is thanks to the power of the Axiom, essentially acting as one enormous library, a wellspring of knowledge, so to speak.

In deciphered illustrations and scrolls, the original Axiom, despite its name denoting one of great intellect, was quite the opposite. The path that evolution took eventually reached the point where those now known as [[The Inhabitants of Luciradis#Terrakin|terrakin]] first gained a level of intelligence found in more human-like races. The original Axiom was but a young terrakin yearning to learn more about Luciradis past their sedentary tribe, having no notable powers themselves.

### The Convergence

**Last Known Ascendant:** [[Astos Yerazel|Ả̷̢̱͒̀s̶̎ͅt̵̻̣́͠ͅo̸̧͐̓͘s̵̥̺͊̀͛ ̴̘̘͑́̎ͅY̷̧̲̪̔̓͠e̵͖̤̯̿r̸̻͝ä̷̧̗́͐z̷̯̅ė̶͎̖̈͜͠l̶͇̼̔͊͜]] 

The Convergence was said to be the original creator of the popularized sundial, giving the people of Luciradis the ability to accurately tell time without having to tediously observe the positions of sun and moon, but rather through the shadows of the sun. The individual behind the title is said to be a master artisan and lauded as the first timekeeper. Due to the popularity of the sundial, the master artisan's services became high in demand, which led the artisan to hire apprentices throughout the land to aid in his work. Soon after, the master artisan left his workshop to travel the lands with a party of adventurers, leaving behind various blueprints and notes to his apprentices.

It is said that during **the Scarring**, the Convergence produced tools of war, including firearms, spears, swords, and accessories of legendary status. These legendary relics are safely stored in various exhibits; though their time has passed, it serves as inspiration for future craftsmen and artisans. The knowledge behind the creation of these tools have been lost to time, but the methods in which to extract the properties of various materials have been passed down to the Ascendants of the Convergence.

Following **the Scarring**, those workshop apprentices built up their names following the legacy of the Convergence, and dispersed throughout Cicatri Ciel to not only further their craft, but also provide for the towns and cities they come across. Thus, in today's Cicatri Ciel, one can find many workshops that laud the name of the apprentice artisans from the Convergence.

## Scholarly Research

The study of the Empyrean Concepts has remained a central field of arcanics throughout the Era of Ascendance and subsequent historical periods.

During the Era of Ascendance, the **Congregation of Academia** contributed substantially to the classification and documentation of Empyrean phenomena. Its investigations established important foundations for understanding essence concentration, variations in magical aptitude, and the identification of Ascendants.

The continued refinement of these principles has been supposed by the **[[Luciradis Authority of Magical Sciences]] (LAMS)**, an institution whose scholarship and standards have remained influential across successive eras.

Within LAMS, the **Division of Arcane Sciences & Research** investigates the fundamental properties of Empyrean phenomena, while the **Division of Applied Thaumaturgy & Engineering** advances the instruments and practical methods through which those properties may be observed and measured.

The **Central Registry of Magical Phenomena** further contributes to the preservations and classification of documented findings, ensuring that significant discoveries remain available for subsequent study.

Nevertheless, the existence of sophisticated measurement techniques and extensive historical records should not be mistaken for a complete understanding of the Empyrean Concepts.

Questions concerning their origins, the circumstances of the Sundering, and the full range of manifestations associated with their essences continue to occupy scholars throughout Luciradis.

As the study of arcanics advances, established interpretations may required reconsideration in light of newly documented phenomena and previously inaccessible historical evidence.
