## Brood of Azharel

### Background

The Brood of Azharel refers to the ruling clan of dragons led by Broodmaster Orsilus of Azharel.

## Dreamweavers (Yumori-te)

### Background

The dreamweavers are one of two **akumari** factions historically associated with the Aurelant, [[The Aurelants#The Reflection|the Reflection]]. They are native to the southern landmass of [[Luciradis#Nova Ciel|Nova Ciel]], make up the majority of the nomadic akumaros, and are the ones who stayed behind while **the Reflection** and **the Illusionists** journeyed north to the landmass of [[Luciradis#Cicatri Ciel|Cicatri Ciel]].

## Ferros Republic

The **[[Ferros Republic]]** is an elven political state centered upon [[Galaderyn]] in eastern Cicatri Ciel. Distinguished by its longstanding traditions of isolationism and hierarchical social organization, the Republic is governed principally by its sovereign class, whose members exercise considerable political and institutional authority.

Despite restrictions on external travel and foreign contact, the Republic maintains limited commercial relationships with neighboring nations and is particularly renowned for its expertise in arcanics and the manufacture of specialized instruments.
