## Wanted Notice

*Date of issue: Undetermined*  

### WARNING

The individual known as the **Ascendant Slayer** is wanted in connection with the deaths of multiple recognized Ascendants throughout [[Luciradis]]. Reports indicate that the perpetrator may possess the ability to wield powers formerly associated with their victims, although the precise nature of these abilities remains unknown.

The identity, whereabouts, and motives of the Ascendant Slayer have yet to be established. Available evidence suggests that the individual deliberately targets recognized Ascendants, with no confirmed pattern of attacks against the general population.

Citizens who encounter an individual suspected of involvement in these crimes are instructed to avoid confrontation and report any relevant information to the nearest authorized patrol of the [[Luxor Citadel]].

Recognized Ascendants are advised to exercise particular caution. Those who believe themselves to be targeted should seek immediate protection from the Congregation of Justice.

All information concerning the Ascendant Slayer shall be treated as a matter of utmost importance to the security of the Citadel and its allied territories.

## Investigative Report

*Date of report: Undetermined*  

The Kingdom of Stalwryn is presently preparing for its annual **Scarring Festival**, an event traditionally accompanied by military demonstrations and organized mock battles between participating nations.

Recent reports indicate the emergence of an unusually capable warrior within Stalwryn, with some accounts suggesting that the individual may possess the powers of an Ascendant. Although these claims remain unverified, the possibility warrants further investigation in light of the continued threat posed by the Ascendant Slayer.

The Congregation will maintain its observation of the festival and investigate any individuals whose abilities or circumstances may be relevant to the ongoing inquiry.

Separately, reports of activity attributed to the Ascendant Slayer have declined considerably since **5263 ASC**. No confirmed sightings or incidents have been recorded in the intervening period, and the individual's present whereabouts remain unknown.

The absence of recent activity should not be interpreted as evidence that the threat has ceased. Until the perpetrator has been identified and apprehended, the Congregation will continue its investigation and maintain appropriate precautions against further attacks upon recognized Ascendants.
