### Early Life

Arzen Lynos was born on Radiant's 17th, 5263 ASC, in [[Lyntoa Village]], a small settlement situated within the territory of the former Kingdom of Stalwryn. Raised among the village's predominantly human population, Arzen enjoyed a relatively peaceful childhood, spending much of his early years exploring the surrounding countryside and participating in the occasional mischief of the local children.

Situated along routes frequented by merchants and travelers, Lyntoa provided its inhabitants with opportunities to encounter peoples and cultures from beyond the kingdom's borders. Arzen developed a particular fascination with these visitors and their accounts of distant lands, gradually nurturing an ambition to explore the wider world himself.

Among the individuals who influenced his upbringing was [[Amara Illustros]], a former traveler who had settled in Lyntoa and served as a caretaker to his family. Arzen developed a close familial attachment to Amara, regarding her as an older sister despite their lack of biological relation.

Having traveled extensively before arriving in Lyntoa, Amara frequently entertained Arzen with accounts of her journeys. Her descriptions of distant civilizations, extraordinary landscapes, and unfamiliar peoples further encouraged his desire for adventure. She also took an active interest in his upbringing, offering guidance and companionship throughout his childhood.

Arzen maintained a close friendship with [[Rietta Aschlera]], an elven girl whose family had settled in Lyntoa to facilitate trade between the Kingdom of Stalwryn and the [[Ferros Republic]]. The two frequently participated in the activities of the village's children, developing a familiarity that would endure long after their eventual departure from their childhood home.

Unbeknownst to Arzen, however, Amara possessed knowledge concerning the circumstances of his birth that would remain concealed from him for many years. The true nature of their relationship, and the consequences of her intervention in his existence, would only become apparent much later in his life.

### The Rite of Continuance

In 5277 ASC, Arzen became embroiled in the circumstances surrounding the [[Rite of Continuance]], an ancient ritual historically performed at Lyntoa's [[Lyntoa Village#Well of Dreams|Well of Dreams]] under an agreement between the village and the royal family of Stalwryn.

The ritual was intended to treat a rare spiritual affliction known as _Soul Dissociation_, historically referred to as _the Waning_. The condition progressively weakened the connection between an individual's soul and physical body, eventually resulting in death if left untreated. Although conventional medicine and arcana could alleviate certain symptoms, neither offered a reliable means of reversing the condition during this period.

The Rite of Continuance offered an extraordinary alternative. Through the sacrifice of a donor, the ritual transferred the deceased individual's soul to the afflicted recipient, reinforcing the connection between the recipient's soul and body. Its performance was exceptionally rare, with only a handful of attempts recorded throughout Stalwryn's history.

The intended beneficiary was [[Karos Varens]], the only son of King [[Leoris Varens]] and heir to the Stalwryn throne. Arzen, then thirteen years of age, was forcibly selected to serve as the donor.

Although his parents opposed the decision, their resistance was tempered by the longstanding obligations of Lyntoa to the monarchy. The village's historical relationship with the royal family, together with the perceived necessity of preserving its heir, placed considerable pressure upon those involved in the ritual's preparations.

The ritual, performed on Horizon's 19th, 5277 ASC, did not proceed as intended.

[[Amara Illustros]], unwilling to permit Arzen's sacrifice, deliberately interfered with its execution. Her intervention was motivated not only by her desire to protect him, but also by an undisclosed objective involving the [[Stairs of Wanderlust]], the realm through which departed souls pass before entering the cycle of reincarnation.

The resulting disruption prevented the ritual from fulfilling its intended purpose. Karos died without receiving the cure, while Amara was likewise presumed to have perished during the incident.

In the aftermath, resentment spread among those who had supported the ritual, particularly over the death of the kingdom's heir and the consequences that might follow for Lyntoa. Arzen, despite having been an unwilling participant, became a target of the resulting hostility.

With his safety no longer assured, several villagers who had opposed the sacrifice secretly arranged his escape. His parents, although similarly opposed to the ritual, remained unaware of these arrangements.

Thus, at the age of thirteen, Arzen departed Lyntoa, leaving behind the home in which he had been raised and the people who had shaped much of his childhood.

Although he harbored resentment toward those responsible for his predicament, Arzen did not extend that animosity to the village as a whole. He remained conscious of those who had opposed the ritual and particularly those whose intervention had enabled his escape.

His departure would ultimately mark the last occasion on which he saw his parents alive.

### Early Travels

Following his escape from Lyntoa, Arzen began traveling through the surrounding territories of Stalwryn. Although he had long wished to explore the world beyond his childhood home, the circumstances of his departure left him with little opportunity to prepare for the journey.

During this period, Arzen began experiencing an unusual sensitivity to the presence of souls. Initially manifesting as faint impressions and indistinct voices, the phenomenon gradually developed into an ability to perceive spiritual presences that ordinarily remained beyond the awareness of the living.

The ability, which Arzen would later describe as _Soul Discernment_, eventually led him to encounter the lingering soul of [[Karos Varens]], whose death had resulted from the failed Rite of Continuance.

Karos had not yet come to terms with his death. Having been raised as the sole heir to the Stalwryn throne, he had spent much of his childhood preparing for responsibilities he would never assume. Although his royal upbringing afforded him considerable privileges, he had long harbored a desire to travel beyond the confines of the capital and experience the world without the restrictions imposed by his position.

Arzen initially regarded the encounter with considerable apprehension. The prince's affliction had been the justification for his own intended sacrifice, and the circumstances surrounding Karos's death remained a source of distress for both.

Nevertheless, the two gradually developed a friendship, discovering a shared fascination with adventure and the lands beyond Stalwryn.

Their companionship offered Karos an opportunity to experience, however indirectly, some measure of the freedom he had desired during his lifetime. For Arzen, the encounter provided companionship during a period of considerable uncertainty following his departure from Lyntoa.

Unlike most wandering souls, Karos remained closely associated with Arzen throughout the years that followed. Their friendship would endure through Arzen's subsequent travels, his service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard, and the events surrounding the kingdom's eventual downfall.

The circumstances of Karos's prolonged presence, and his eventual departure from the living world, would later become subjects of particular interest to those investigating Arzen's unusual abilities.

### Journey to Merastel

In the years following his departure from Lyntoa, Arzen traveled throughout the southern territories of Stalwryn, supporting himself through occasional work and making use of the hunting and survival skills he had acquired during his childhood. Although the circumstances of his departure had been far from ideal, his travels nevertheless afforded him opportunities to pursue the curiosity and adventurous interests he had developed from youth.

During this period, Arzen remained accompanied by the lingering soul of [[Karos Varens]], whose presence provided companionship throughout his travels. Their shared interest in exploration frequently brought them to unfamiliar settlements and regions, although Karos's inability to interact directly with the physical world occasionally complicated their endeavors.

While traveling near the outskirts of the [[Port of Anahymn]], Arzen encountered [[Saria Pulse]], a young woman of draconic descent who had recently fled her homeland in the [[Grandiose Peaks]]. Saria was being pursued by individuals associated with Azharel, who sought to return her to the responsibilities she had abandoned following her succession as Ascendant of the [[The Aurelants#The Expanse|Expanse]].

Recognizing her predicament, Arzen intervened and assisted Saria in evading her pursuers. He subsequently helped conceal her presence within Anahymn, where her unusual appearance might otherwise have attracted unwanted attention.

Despite their unfamiliarity with one another, the two discovered a shared desire to explore the world beyond the places in which they had been raised. Arzen's curiosity and relatively easygoing disposition complemented Saria's more outspoken personality, and their initial acquaintance gradually developed into a friendship.

The two eventually decided to travel together toward [[Merastel]], the capital of Stalwryn. According to Saria's account, their journey lasted approximately six weeks, during which they encountered considerable difficulty securing sufficient provisions and suitable accommodations.

With limited funds between them, Arzen frequently relied upon his experience in hunting and gathering, while Saria demonstrated exceptional skill in cooking and preparing meals from whatever ingredients they could scour. Their circumstances required them to cooperate closely, gradually establishing a familiarity that extended beyond their shared interest in adventure.

During their travels, Arzen also disclosed his unusual ability to perceive lingering souls, including that of his longtime companion, Karos. Saria initially regarded his claims with considerable skepticism, occasionally questioning whether Arzen was entirely serious about the existence of his supposed companion.

Although she gradually came to accept that Arzen was experiencing phenomena beyond her own perception, her inability to see or communicate with Karos directly remained a source of occasional amusement between them.

As their journey continued, Arzen and Saria became increasingly comfortable in one another's company. Their friendship gradually developed into mutual affection, although neither immediately expressed an intention to pursue a more intimate relationship.

Upon reaching Merastel, the pair initially sought accommodation at various inns while considering how best to establish themselves within the capital. Their arrival marked the beginning of a new chapter in their lives, one that would eventually lead both into the service of the Stalwryn monarchy.

### Life in the Emberlit City

Upon arriving in [[Merastel]], Arzen and Saria initially struggled to establish themselves within the unfamiliar capital. Although the city offered considerably greater opportunities than the settlements they had encountered during their travels, their limited financial resources left them dependent upon temporary accommodations and occasional work.

Despite these difficulties, Arzen quickly developed an appreciation for Merastel's bustling streets, extensive markets, and distinctive architecture. The city's numerous bridges and elevated districts presented a considerable contrast to the rural surroundings of his childhood, while its thriving workshops and commercial establishments offered frequent opportunities to satisfy his curiosity.

Their circumstances changed following an altercation involving several members of the Stalwryn Royal Guard.

While traversing one of the city's commercial districts, Arzen and Saria encountered a young feline Terrakin girl, later identified as [[Kallie Myrai]], who was being subjected to harassment by a patrol of royal knights. The circumstances preceding the confrontation remain poorly documented, although surviving accounts suggest that the soldiers' conduct was motivated, at least in part, by prevailing prejudices against nonhuman inhabitants.

Arzen intervened on Kallie's behalf, drawing the attention and hostility of the knights. Saria subsequently became involved when the soldiers directed similar remarks toward her draconic appearance. What began as a verbal confrontation escalated into violence, culminating in Arzen physically assaulting several members of the patrol.

Both Arzen and Saria were subsequently brought before King [[Leoris Varens]] to answer for their involvement in the incident. Given the severity of the offense and the considerable authority exercised by the Royal Guard, the pair had little reason to expect leniency.

Contrary to these expectations, Leoris personally intervened and granted them a pardon. Although the precise reasoning behind his decision was not formally recorded, contemporary accounts describe his treatment of the two as unusually generous, particularly in light of his reputation for severity during the earlier years of his reign.

The king subsequently extended financial assistance from his personal funds, enabling Arzen and Saria to purchase a modest property within Merastel's Lower Districts. There, the pair established an eatery known as [[Merastel#The Kindled Hearth|The Kindled Hearth]], initially operating on a small scale while gradually developing a reputation for its welcoming atmosphere and quality of food.

Saria's considerable culinary abilities contributed greatly to the establishment's growing popularity, while Arzen assisted with its daily operations and the procurement of ingredients. Their shared responsibilities afforded them a degree of stability that neither had experienced during their earlier travels, and their developing affection gradually matured into a committed relationship.

The Kindled Hearth eventually became a well-regarded establishment among the capital's residents, attracting patrons from beyond its immediate neighborhood. For Arzen in particular, the eatery represented more than a means of livelihood. Having left Lyntoa under circumstances beyond his control, he had finally found a place where he could build a life according to his own wishes.

His acquaintance with Kallie also endured beyond their initial encounter. The young Terrakin gradually became a familiar presence in the lives of Arzen and Saria, establishing a friendship that would survive the destruction of Stalwryn and continue through the subsequent eras of Luciradis.

Although neither had initially intended to pursue a military career, Arzen and Saria eventually sought to repay Leoris for his generosity by applying to serve in the Stalwryn Royal Guard. Their decision marked the beginning of their formal involvement with the monarchy and would ultimately bring Arzen into considerably closer association with the king.

Their subsequent service would also see Arzen inherit the powers of the [[The Aurelants#The Paragon|Paragon]], a development that would fundamentally alter both his responsibilities and his place within the kingdom.

### Physical Combat

Before Arzen came to the [[Merastel]], he used to sport a **double-ended glaive** cobbled together with rudimentary materials such as sharpened stone and wood. Even with such primitive materials, Arzen was unusually adept with the weapon, and has proven to us his hunting skills. By [[Leoris Varens|King Varens]]' order, the finest blacksmith forged a much stronger version of Arzen's glaive, made with lightest and sharpest steel combined with a more practical grip infused with elven magicks. This version allows Arzen to split the glaive in half, and dual-wield two longswords.

Despite this, however, Arzen refused to use the new glaive, instead opting to learn the **way of the sword and shield**. While we had no comment from the boy himself, [[Saria Pulse|Saria]] tells us it's because Arzen wanted repay the debt he owes to the King for saving him. Coincidentally, it is for the very same reason that Saria pursues the path of a lancer vanguard. She notes, however, that there may be a time where Arzen's time as a knight would come to an end, and the debt to the King be fulfilled. When that time comes, Arzen may return to using the glaive and continue his journey outside the Kingdom.

Arzen was passed down the remaining remnants of the [[The Aurelants#The Paragon|Paragon]] through King Varens, and thus was made its Ascendant. Its power had manifested such that it bestowed Arzen complete mastery over his body, allowing it to use even the most taxing of magicks. Naturally, this power also gives Arzen access to the most powerful defensive magicks. Our scholars still do not know why Arzen had developed such blessings from being the Paragon's Ascendant, though we can only guess that something from his past triggered this extreme change.

During his time with the knights, Arzen wears a stripped-down version of the elite knight's armor, opting for more maneuverability and flexibility in combat. The elite knight's armor, for reference, is constructed using [[Catalog of Materials#Caladonic Steel|caladonic steel]] with varying layers of metal plates. Despite this, the armor molds to the shape of the wearer. Arzen also uses a caladonic steel longsword and a caladonic steel shield laced with magicks that bear striking similarity with that of the [[The Aurelants#The Horizon|Horizon]]. He mostly uses the magic within to materialize the sword and shield out of nowhere, as if wishing it into existence. With both the Paragon's power and our rigorous knight training, Arzen proves a formidable warrior with his offensive and defensive capabilities.

#### Obsidian Might

Tapping into the _Root of Earth_, Arzen is able to manifest and control extremely durable **blackstone** shards, or **[[Catalog of Materials#Obsidian|obsidian]]** in alchemist terms. Obsidian is normally a very fragile and brittle material, only used in crafting accessories and certain sharp weapons. However, through the Paragon's power, Arzen is able to draw, from the Root of Earth, the extremely durable properties of **[[Catalog of Materials#Caladon|caladon]]**, and infuse it into the obsidian, creating a material that is both virtually unbreakable and dangerously sharp.
