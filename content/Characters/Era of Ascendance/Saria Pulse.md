## Background

> *“There is an ancient draconic proverb: ‘To taste freedom, spread your wings.’ I have always believed that these words hold true for all life on Luciradis. No one should be bound by their doubts, their suffering, or the expectations of others. We all deserve the freedom to pursue our own paths, no matter the burdens we carry. And if those burdens become too heavy to bear alone, I would gladly lend my wings to anyone who needs them.”*
> 
> — Saria Pulse

Saria is the daughter of [[Factions of Luciradis#Brood of Arodraenix|Orsilus of Arodraenix]], the reigning draconic sovereign of the skies above the [[Grandiose Peaks]], and a human woman whose identity remains unknown.Their union gave rise to Saria, the first dragon-human hybrid in the history of Luciradis, whose unprecedented birth marked the beginning of the people who would eventually become known as Vyrians.

Although the identity of Saria's mother has eluded investigators, recent findings suggest an affiliation with [[Gensōkyo]]. These findings are supported by historical accounts of a dragon repeatedly descending upon the [[Amber Clifftops]], though the precise circumstances of these visits remain uncertain.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Although dragons have long been regarded as an established part of Luciradis, it is worth remembering that their origins lie beyond our world. Their extraordinary longevity is likewise well documented, with certain individuals surviving for several millennia.
> 
> In the millennia following Saria's birth, other dragon-human hybrids emerged, eventually forming the population now recognized as **Vyrians**. Their lifespans, however, are considerably shorter than those of their draconic ancestors, averaging approximately 170 years.
> 
> Saria represents a notable exception. Despite her predominantly human appearance, she appears to have inherited the longevity of a full-blooded dragon, a characteristic that remains poorly understood.

Saria is the current Ascendant of the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Expanse|Expanse]], having succeeded her father, Orsilus, as its bearer. Among the dragons of Arodraenix, succession is governed by an ancient tradition intended to preserve the power of the Expanse within their lineage. A prospective successor must first complete a trial before undergoing the ritual through which the power is passed from one Ascendant to the next.

Although Saria successfully completed both the trial and the succession ritual, she struggled to reconcile her newfound power with the responsibilities expected of its bearer. Overwhelmed by these expectations, she eventually fled the [[Grandiose Peaks]] at a young age, leaving behind her family and the life that had been prepared for her.

Following her departure from the Grandiose Peaks, Saria eventually found herself near the outskirts of the [[Port of Anahymn]], where she first encountered [[Arzen Lynos]]. Pursued by individuals from Arodraenix seeking her return, she was rescued by Arzen, who helped conceal her presence within the predominantly human port city.

The two subsequently departed for the [[Stalwryn Kingdom]], undertaking a journey that, according to Saria's own account, lasted approximately six weeks. With little money between them, they were forced to provide for themselves along the way, gathering food and securing whatever provisions they could. These shared hardships marked the beginning of a companionship that would come to define much of their lives.

Upon arriving in the [[Stalwryn Kingdom]], Saria and Arzen initially took residence in various inns while attempting to establish themselves within the capital. During this period, the two became involved in an altercation with a patrol of royal knights, who reportedly expressed considerable hostility toward Saria on account of her unfamiliar origins and draconic appearance. Arzen intervened in her defense, ultimately resorting to violence against the knights.

Under ordinary circumstances, such an offense would have carried severe penalties. However, in a decision considered uncharacteristically lenient by those familiar with his reign, King [[Leoris Varens]] personally intervened, pardoning the pair and extending financial assistance from his own funds. With this support, Saria and Arzen were able to purchase property within the capital and establish an eatery known as [[Stalwryn Kingdom#The Campfire|the Campfire]], which would eventually become a popular establishment among the city's residents.

Following the establishment of the Campfire, Saria and Arzen sought to repay King Varens for his generosity by applying to serve in the Stalwryn Royal Guard. It was during this process that Saria formally adopted the surname **Pulse**, choosing to distance herself from the familial name she had long since rejected.

According to Saria's own account, the name was intended to commemorate the companionship she had forged with Arzen throughout their travels. It also held a more personal significance, alluding to her heightened perception of heartbeats through the power of the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Expanse|Expanse]]. In choosing the name Pulse, Saria sought to establish an identity defined not by her ancestry, but by the life and relationships she had chosen for herself.

Following her induction into the Stalwryn Royal Guard, Saria quickly distinguished herself through her exceptional proficiency with the spear and her aggressive, highly mobile approach to combat. Her abilities earned her rapid advancement through the military ranks, culminating in her appointment as commander of the kingdom's vanguard unit.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Following the fall of the Stalwryn Kingdom, Saria's tenure as commander of the vanguard came to an end, along with the military institution she had served for many years.
> 
> It is worth noting that King Varens had previously expressed a willingness to relieve both Saria and Arzen of their duties, recognizing their shared desire to explore the world beyond the kingdom's borders. Although their service ultimately ended under considerably different circumstances, his intentions suggest that neither had been expected to remain bound to their military obligations indefinitely.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> In the millennia following the fall of Stalwryn, Saria eventually departed Luciradis to pursue her longstanding desire to explore the unknown. Although the precise date of her departure remains uncertain, surviving accounts indicate that she traveled alone, journeying between distant worlds and encountering civilizations far removed from her own.
> 
> Her travels continued for an indeterminate period before she returned to Luciradis shortly after the outbreak of the conflict with the Horizon, where she would lend her strength to the ensuing struggle.

## Appearance

![[saria-refsheet.webp]]*Illustration by @8Ovjfg8YRwM7qXz*

Despite her draconic heritage, Saria possesses a predominantly humanoid appearance, characterized by pale skin, long, flowing platinum-blonde hair with natural red highlights, and striking teal eyes. Her physique is athletic yet curvaceous, reflecting the physical conditioning acquired throughout her years of service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard.

Her draconic ancestry is most apparent in the pair of black horns extending from her head, the patches of pitch-black scales adorning portions of her skin, and a long tail reaching approximately the length of her legs. Vibrantly colored fins protrude near the base of her tail, providing a distinctive contrast to her otherwise dark draconic features.

Saria also bears a permanent enchanted marking near her abdomen, commonly known as a **love crest** or **womb crest**. Such markings are primarily associated with red-light districts and illicit enchantment practices, where they are employed to heighten sexual desire and induce submissiveness toward an individual designated through the enchantment. Their capacity to influence the wearer's autonomy has made them a subject of considerable controversy.

Unlike many documented recipients of these enchantments, Saria acquired her crest voluntarily and with knowledge of its intended effects. She makes little effort to conceal her fondness for the marking, regarding it as an expression of her personal desires rather than a source of shame.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Despite the passage of several millennia, Saria continues to retain the appearance of a human woman in her mid-to-late twenties. Whether this remarkable preservation of youth is an inherent characteristic of her unusual draconic ancestry or the result of other factors remains uncertain.
> 
> I have considered asking her about the matter directly, though I suspect she would find such inquiries rather intrusive. Some questions, I believe, are better left unanswered.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> In the present era, Saria continues to wear the same uniform she once donned during her service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard. Despite the millennia that have passed, she has maintained it in remarkably good condition, a testament to the considerable care she devotes to its preservation.
> 
> Curiously, its distinctive design remains compatible with contemporary fashion in Nova Ciel, allowing her to wear it comfortably in both everyday and more formal settings. Although Saria enjoys experimenting with newer styles, she frequently returns to her old uniform, citing both familiarity and comfort as reasons for her preference.

Saria possesses the ability to assume a fully draconic form of formidable proportions, with a wingspan reaching approximately **60 meters (197 feet)**. In this state, much of her body is covered in brilliant white scales, sharply contrasting with the pitch-black scales lining her underside. The latter retain the same coloration as those visible upon her humanoid form.

As the Ascendant of the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Expanse|Expanse]], Saria's draconic wings exhibit a distinctive violet coloration, a characteristic consistent with ancient depictions of the Concept's power. When invoking **Shimmering Call** in this form, the violet coloration shifts to a brilliant gold. Saria then extends her wings to their full breadth, producing a striking display associated with the rallying power of the Expanse.

## Personality

During her years in the Stalwryn Kingdom, Saria was widely regarded as a compassionate, carefree, and approachable individual. Her playful disposition was frequently accompanied by a disarming frankness, as she seldom hesitated to speak her mind, even when doing so risked causing offense. Despite naturally attracting considerable attention, she was often uncomfortable with being placed at its center.

Her demeanor changed considerably when circumstances demanded seriousness, particularly during her service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard. As commander of the kingdom's vanguard, Saria demonstrated a disciplined and commanding presence, holding both herself and her subordinates to exacting standards. Though generally easygoing outside her duties, she was uncompromising in matters of military preparedness and the welfare of those under her command.

Saria was notably less forthcoming when questioned about her departure from Arodraenix. She generally dismissed inquiries concerning her family and upbringing, maintaining that such matters belonged to a past she had little desire to revisit.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> The Saria described in the original Stalwryn records differs considerably from the individual I came to know in subsequent eras. Over the course of several millennia, repeated exposure to conflict, loss, and the calamities that befell Luciradis gradually tempered her once-carefree disposition. In its place emerged a more reserved and occasionally jaded outlook, though her compassion for others remained largely unchanged.
> 
> Despite these experiences, Saria continued to uphold an optimistic perspective, even when circumstances offered little reason for it. I would hesitate to describe this as naïveté. Rather, it was a conviction she maintained in spite of all she had witnessed, and perhaps one of the few things she refused to surrender to the passage of time.
> 
> Following the conclusion of the conflict with the Horizon, however, I have observed a remarkable change in her demeanor. It is as though a burden she had carried for millennia has finally been lifted. The seriousness that once characterized her disposition has given way to a renewed sense of ease, reminiscent of the carefree individual described in the earliest accounts of her life.
> 
> She now takes considerable pleasure in the ordinary experiences afforded by life in Nova Ciel, whether frequenting restaurants and cafés, spending time with friends, or indulging in her love of cooking. What might otherwise seem like unremarkable pursuits appear to hold particular significance for her. For the first time in a very long while, Saria seems content to enjoy the present without concerning herself with whatever calamity might await beyond it.

### Likes
- **Gourmet food** — Saria possesses a considerable appreciation for fine cuisine, both as a cook and an enthusiast.
- **Fashion** — Despite her attachment to her old vanguard uniform, Saria maintains an active interest in contemporary fashion.
- **Exploration** — Saria has long possessed an insatiable curiosity for unfamiliar places and cultures.

### Dislikes
- **Self-destructive attitudes** — Saria finds it difficult to stand by while others diminish their own worth or disregard the concern of those who care for them. Though sympathetic toward individuals struggling with self-doubt, she can become frustrated when her attempts to help are repeatedly dismissed. Her impatience stems not from contempt, but from a sincere desire to see others recognize the value of their own lives.
- **Loneliness** — Despite her willingness to travel alone, Saria harbors a deep-seated fear of enduring life without meaningful companionship. Having outlived countless individuals throughout her long existence, she is painfully aware of how fleeting her relationships with shorter-lived peoples can be. More than solitude itself, she fears the prospect of having no one with whom to share her experiences, celebrate her joys, or seek comfort during moments of vulnerability.

## Expertise

### Martial Combat

Saria is a highly accomplished swordspear wielder, having refined her abilities through years of service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard. Her fighting style incorporates traditional lance techniques taught by veteran soldiers of the kingdom, alongside a more unconventional approach developed through her travels and training with [[Arzen Lynos]]. The resulting style favors aggressive engagements, precise strikes, and rapid maneuvering, allowing her to exploit openings in an opponent's defenses.

Her primary weapon is a swordspear traditionally awarded to distinguished members of the Stalwryn Royal Guard upon surpassing their instructors in combat. Saria's weapon was subsequently modified to accommodate the channeling of her innate draconic magic, allowing her to envelop its blade in violet flames. By continuously supplying her own magical energy, she can considerably enhance the weapon's destructive potential, inflicting both physical and thermal damage with each strike.

During her tenure as a vanguard commander, Saria frequently favored mobility over conventional protection, removing portions of her standard-issue armor in favor of the lighter uniform beneath. This allowed her to capitalize on her exceptional agility while maintaining the freedom of movement demanded by her aggressive fighting style.

The distinctive white coloration of her uniform also served a secondary purpose. In contrast to the muted metal armor worn by ordinary soldiers, Saria's appearance made her readily distinguishable on the battlefield, complementing the rallying capabilities associated with the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Expanse|Expanse]]. By drawing attention to herself, she could more effectively serve as a focal point for the soldiers fighting alongside her.

### Arcana

Although Saria's combat expertise is primarily rooted in her martial abilities, she is also a capable practitioner of arcana. Her proficiency is particularly apparent in her manipulation of fire, through which she incorporates her innate draconic energy into conventional invocations. This allows her to produce magical effects that differ considerably from those achieved through ordinary elemental manipulation.

#### Violet Flames

**Violet Flames** is a specialized adaptation of the fundamental _Create: Fire_ invocation, distinguished by Saria's infusion of her innate draconic energy into the resulting flames. This infusion produces their characteristic violet coloration and grants them an intensity considerably greater than that of conventional fire invocations.

The technique is most frequently employed to envelop the blade of her swordspear in fire, combining the weapon's cutting force with intense heat. However, its applications extend beyond weapon augmentation, as Saria can manifest and manipulate these flames independently, adapting their form and intensity to suit the circumstances of an engagement.

Although Violet Flames retains the versatility of conventional fire arcana, its exceptional properties are a consequence of Saria's draconic heritage rather than the invocation itself.

### Ascendant Abilities

As the Ascendant of the [[Empyrean Concepts#The Expanse|Expanse]], Saria possesses extraordinary physical capabilities that extend beyond those afforded by her draconic heritage. By drawing upon the Concept's power, she can greatly enhance her strength, agility, and endurance, complementing the aggressive and highly mobile fighting style she developed during her service in the Stalwryn Royal Guard.

The Expanse also allows Saria to overcome certain limitations inherent to her mixed ancestry. While Vyrians generally experience considerable difficulty assuming the full draconic forms of their ancestors, Saria is capable of sustaining such a transformation for extended periods. In her humanoid form, she can additionally manifest ethereal wings, granting her flight without requiring a complete transformation.

#### Shimmering Call

**Shimmering Call** is an ability exclusive to the Ascendant of the Expanse, allowing Saria to issue a rallying cry that bolsters the morale, strength, and endurance of those within its reach. Its effects extend beyond mere encouragement, imparting a tangible enhancement to the physical capabilities of her allies.

In her humanoid form, the call can affect an area comparable to a small township, with the [[Port of Anahymn]] serving as an approximate measure of its effective range. When performed in her draconic form, its reach increases substantially, allowing her cry to extend across the positions of several battalions.

The invocation also produces a distinctive visual manifestation when performed in her draconic form. Her normally violet wings turn a brilliant gold as she extends them to their full breadth, creating a striking display that complements the rallying effects of the call.

#### Draconic Ascension

**Draconic Ascension** is Saria's complete transformation into her draconic form, facilitated and sustained through the power of the Expanse. Although transformation is an inherent characteristic of her draconic ancestry, her mixed heritage would ordinarily make assuming such a form considerably more difficult. The Expanse allows her to overcome this limitation, maintaining the transformation for extended periods without the same restrictions experienced by other Vyrians.

In this state, Saria abandons her swordspear in favor of her natural weapons, employing her immense physical strength, claws, and draconic flames in combat. Her greater size and physical capabilities allow her to engage opponents on a substantially larger scale, while her innate draconic magic continues to augment her attacks.

Draconic Ascension also enhances the effectiveness of her other abilities, most notably Shimmering Call, whose range increases considerably in this form. Her violet flames likewise remain available to her, allowing Saria to envelop her claws in fire or unleash them through her draconic breath.
