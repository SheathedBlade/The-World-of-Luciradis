**Amara Illustros** was a former Beacon of the [[Information/Peoples & Societies/Factions/index#Luxori Covenant|Luxori Covenant]] who, following her excommunication, traveled throughout Luciradis before settling in [[Lyntoa Village]] within the Kingdom of Stalwryn. Born into an influential Luxori family, she abandoned her former affiliations and established a quieter life as the caretaker and mentor of [[Arzen Lynos]], whom she regarded as a younger brother.

Amara was remembered for her compassion toward those under her care and her determination to protect them from the hardships she had witnessed during her travels. She also possessed considerable martial expertise, introducing Arzen to the twinblade techniques that would influence his later development as a combatant.

Her apparent death during the failed [[Rite of Continuance]] in 5277 ASC brought an end to her known activities. Subsequent investigations, however, revealed that her involvement in the events surrounding Lyntoa extended far beyond what contemporary records had suggested.

## Background

> _"Isn't it the case that we want future generations to have better lives than those of their predecessors? Then, what I have done is simple, really. I strive for a future wherein those who come after us would put surviving as an afterthought. The path I have chosen to take would always be paved in ruin and tragedy._
> 
> _My only regret is that you have chosen to shoulder my legacy as your burden. But if you would consider all that I have done, for you or otherwise, sins, then I'll gladly be a sinner."_
> 
> — Amara Illustros

### Early Life and the Luxori Covenant

Amara was born into the Illustros family, an influential household within [[Luxor Citadel]] whose members maintained longstanding ties to the Citadel's governing institutions and military affairs.

Her upbringing placed her within a society deeply influenced by the teachings of the Luxori Covenant. Amara eventually entered the Covenant's service, attaining the position of **Beacon**, a religious vocation that involved travel beyond the Citadel and contact with communities throughout Luciradis.

The position exposed her to societies whose circumstances differed considerably from those of her homeland. While the precise extent of her travels remains poorly documented, her experiences brought her into contact with both the achievements of civilization and the suffering endured by those excluded from its prosperity.

Over time, these encounters would influence her understanding of the world and the convictions for which she subsequently became known.

### Excommunication and Travels

Amara's association with the Luxori Covenant ended following her excommunication. The circumstances surrounding this decision remain uncertain, with surviving institutional records offering little explanation for her dismissal.

Her estrangement from the Covenant also damaged her relationship with the Illustros family. Having relinquished her former position and severed ties with her household, Amara departed Luxor Citadel and continued traveling independently.

During this period, she retained aspects of her former religious identity, at times presenting herself as a traveling sister. Whether this practice served primarily to conceal her past or reflected a continued attachment to her former vocation remains unclear.

Although Amara seldom spoke of the experiences that followed her departure, her travels appear to have reinforced a growing dissatisfaction with the institutions and conflicts that shaped life throughout Luciradis.

Eventually, she arrived in [[Lyntoa Village]], a small coastal settlement within the Kingdom of Stalwryn, where she established a more permanent residence.

### Life in Lyntoa

In Lyntoa, Amara became a caretaker to the family of [[Arzen Lynos]], developing a close relationship with the boy and his parents.

Arzen regarded her as an older sister, despite their lack of acknowledged biological relation. Having traveled extensively before settling in the village, Amara frequently entertained him with accounts of distant lands, unfamiliar peoples, and experiences beyond Stalwryn's borders.

Her stories encouraged his curiosity about the world, while her guidance helped shape his early understanding of the people who inhabited it. Amara's desire to show him the wonders she had encountered stood in contrast to her reluctance to discuss the suffering that had accompanied many of her travels.

She also introduced Arzen to martial combat, teaching him the fundamentals of the twinblade. Although his early practice weapons were rudimentary, her instruction gave him a foundation upon which he would later develop his own techniques.

To those who knew her in Lyntoa, Amara was a familiar presence within the household, recognized for her care toward Arzen and her willingness to share the knowledge she had acquired during her travels.

Few were aware of the circumstances surrounding her earlier life, and fewer still suspected the extraordinary nature of her relationship with the boy she had helped raise.

### The Final Rite of Continuance

On Horizon's 19th, 5277 ASC, Amara intervened in the [[Rite of Continuance]] conducted at Lyntoa's [[Lyntoa Village#Well of Dreams|Well of Dreams]].

The ritual had been arranged to preserve the life of Prince [[Karos Varens]], the afflicted heir to the Stalwryn throne. Arzen, then thirteen years of age, had been selected against his wishes to serve as the donor whose soul would be sacrificed.

Unwilling to permit his death, Amara deliberately disrupted the ritual. Her intervention prevented its intended completion, resulting in Karos's death and her own apparent demise.

Contemporary records subsequently treated Amara as deceased. The circumstances of her continued existence, and the reasons underlying her intervention, would remain concealed for millennia.

## Appearance

## Personality

Amara possessed an outwardly warm and approachable disposition, particularly toward children and individuals placed under her care. She enjoyed sharing stories of her travels and encouraging curiosity about unfamiliar places, often preferring to direct conversations toward the experiences and ambitions of others rather than discuss her own past.

Her time as a Beacon had exposed her to suffering that continued to influence her outlook long after she abandoned the Covenant. These experiences fostered a deep concern for those whose lives were constrained by circumstances beyond their control. She was particularly sensitive to the vulnerability of younger generations, believing that they deserved opportunities denied to those who came before them.

Yet Amara was far from an unquestioning optimist. Beneath her gentler manner lay a growing disillusionment with the institutions and powers that governed Luciradis. She questioned whether the peace maintained by its civilizations represented genuine progress or merely concealed suffering that successive generations had come to accept.

Once convinced of a particular course of action, Amara could be extraordinarily difficult to dissuade. Her willingness to assume responsibility for her decisions was accompanied by a readiness to determine what sacrifices she considered necessary, even when those decisions denied others the freedom she professed to value.

This contradiction was especially evident in her relationship with Arzen. She encouraged him to discover the world for himself, yet attempted to shield him from its cruelties and made decisions concerning his existence without his knowledge.

Her affection for him was sincere. So, too, was her conviction that the world needed to change.

Later investigations would reveal how far she had been prepared to pursue that conviction.

## Expertise

### Martial Combat

Amara was an accomplished martial combatant specializing in the use of a **twinblade**, a double-ended weapon that could be separated into two independently wielded swords. Her proficiency reflected the martial instruction she received during her service as a Beacon of the Luxori Covenant, whose duties occasionally required travel through dangerous territories.

Her weapon originated as a ceremonial armament associated with her former position. More ornate than conventional military equipment, its blades and central fittings bore decorative elements reflecting the traditions of the Covenant. Despite its ceremonial design, the twinblade remained fully functional in combat, carefully balanced for both its joined and separated configurations.

Amara's fighting style emphasized control, fluid transitions, and the ability to adapt to an opponent's movements. In its joined configuration, the weapon permitted sweeping attacks and rapid changes in direction. When separated, its individual blades allowed her to engage at closer distances with greater freedom of movement.

Following her departure from the Covenant, Amara incorporated an articulated mechanism into the blades, allowing their segments to extend into flexible configurations resembling the whip-swords employed by certain warriors associated with [[Gensōkyo]]. She rarely employed this modification in ordinary combat, reserving it for circumstances in which conventional attacks would prove difficult to execute.

During her years in [[Lyntoa Village]], Amara introduced [[Arzen Lynos]] to the fundamentals of twinblade combat. Her instruction emphasized balance, coordination, and the relationship between the weapon's different configurations.

Arzen subsequently developed a fighting style of his own, incorporating the techniques he learned from Amara into the more aggressive approach that would characterize his early travels.

Although he later received formal military training and adopted other weapons during his service to Stalwryn, his familiarity with the twinblade remained rooted in her instruction.

### Arcana

Amara possessed an exceptional affinity for the [[The Aurelants#The Horizon|Horizon]], although the true extent of her capabilities remained unknown to those who encountered her during her travels and residence in Lyntoa.

Her abilities were not publicly recognized during this period, and surviving contemporary accounts provide little reliable information concerning her ordinary applications of arcana.

Subsequent investigations established that Amara possessed extraordinary concentrations of alluvia associated with several Authorities, including the Horizon, Resonance, Radiant, and Convergence. Her capabilities extended beyond what could ordinarily be inferred from her apparent circumstances or former vocation.

The methods through which she acquired and employed these powers are examined in the supplementary findings below.

## Subsequent Investigations

_The following findings incorporate evidence and testimony obtained after Amara Illustros's recorded death in 5277 ASC. They concern circumstances that remained unknown to contemporary investigators and were subsequently brought to light through the expedition to Cicatri Ciel._

### The Ascendant Slayer

Later investigations established that Amara Illustros was the individual once known throughout Luciradis as the **[[The Ascendant Slayer|Ascendant Slayer]]**, an unidentified assailant responsible for the deaths of multiple recognized Ascendants.

Historical reports issued by the Congregation of Justice had documented a pattern of attacks against Ascendants, accompanied by accounts suggesting that the perpetrator possessed abilities associated with their victims. Despite extensive investigations, the authorities failed to establish the assailant's identity.

Amara's subsequent revelations confirmed that the attacks formed part of a deliberate effort to gather alluvia associated with the Aurelants' Authorities. Through the deaths of other Ascendants and methods whose precise workings remain incompletely understood, she accumulated powers ordinarily distributed among separate individuals.

According to testimony concerning her activities, Amara had acquired exceptional concentrations of alluvia associated with the [[The Aurelants#The Resonance|Resonance]], [[The Aurelants#The Radiant|Radiant]], and [[The Aurelants#The Convergence|Convergence]]. These acquisitions contributed to her ability to pursue manifestations beyond those typically associated with her natural affinity.

Her activities were interrupted for reasons that remained unknown to contemporary investigators, with documented incidents attributed to the Ascendant Slayer declining substantially after 5263 ASC.

The identification of Amara as the perpetrator also raised new questions concerning her actions during the intervening years, particularly her decision to settle in Lyntoa and the circumstances surrounding Arzen's existence.

### The Division of Amara's Soul

Amara's apparent death during the failed Rite of Continuance did not result in the complete disappearance of her soul.

Subsequent findings established that the disruption of the ritual had divided her spiritual existence, with different aspects of her consciousness continuing independently.

One portion, associated with her compassion and the qualities remembered by those who knew her in Lyntoa, became incorporated into the soul of **[[Leoris Varens]]**, the reigning monarch of Stalwryn.

This union contributed to changes in Leoris's disposition that had previously been difficult to reconcile with contemporary accounts of his earlier rule. Although these changes were observable, their connection to Amara remained unknown until much later.

Another portion of her soul persisted within the **[[Lyntoa Village#Well of Dreams|Well of Dreams]]**, sustained through its association with the Horizon's alluvium. This remnant retained the convictions that had driven Amara's efforts to reshape Luciradis, becoming increasingly bound to the extraordinary circumstances surrounding the Well.

The surviving remnant eventually became associated with the original [[The Aurelants#The Horizon|Aurelant of the Horizon]], whose influence contributed to the conflict later encountered by the expedition to Cicatri Ciel.

Despite their differing manifestations, the surviving aspects of Amara retained her attachment to Arzen and the desire that had shaped much of her earlier life: to see him experience a world beyond the confines of the village in which he had been raised.

The division of her soul consequently complicated attempts to understand her as a single, continuous individual. Neither the compassion remembered by her companions nor the destructive convictions revealed through later investigations could be dismissed as entirely separate from the woman Amara had been.

### Arzen's Origins and Immortality

Among the most consequential revelations arising from the expedition was Amara's role in the creation of **[[Arzen Lynos]]**.

During her residence in Lyntoa, Amara had grown close to the family for whom she served as a caretaker. When the family suffered the loss of a child before birth, she intervened through an extraordinary application of the Horizon's Authority.

Amara's actions brought Arzen into existence, granting him a life that would otherwise have been lost.

This intervention, however, carried a condition whose consequences neither Arzen nor his family understood.

**Arzen's continued existence was bound to Amara's own, rendering him immortal for as long as she continued to exist in some form.**

The condition was not merely an extraordinary enhancement of his physical resilience. It established a dependence between their existences that persisted beyond Amara's apparent death and the division of her soul.

The pact endured beyond Amara's apparent death. Even after her soul divided, the remnants of her consciousness continued to sustain the condition that bound Arzen's life to hers.

Amara had raised him as a younger brother, concealing both his origins and the terms of his immortality. Her affection was genuine, but she had made a choice concerning his existence without his knowledge or consent.

The discovery cast Arzen's extraordinary longevity in a different light. What had long appeared to be an inexplicable condition was now understood to have originated in Amara's intervention before his birth.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> I have often found that the most difficult historical judgments concern actions undertaken for reasons we might otherwise admire.
> 
> Amara wished to spare a family the loss of a child, and the life she brought into being has since touched countless others. Yet the condition attached to that gift was neither explained nor offered as a choice.
> 
> Whatever her intentions, Arzen was left to bear the consequences of a decision made before he could understand it, much less refuse it.

### The Expedition and the Second Scarring

The expedition to Cicatri Ciel eventually uncovered Amara's surviving spiritual remnant within the Well of Dreams. What began as an investigation into the abandoned ritual site brought the expedition team into confrontation with Amara and the original Aurelant of the Horizon.

Their conflict, subsequently remembered as the **Second Scarring**, revealed the extent of Amara's ambitions. Having accumulated alluvia from several Authorities and manipulated Arzen's extraordinary capabilities, she sought to remake Luciradis and end the Aurelants' influence over future generations.

Despite these preparations, Amara's attempt to reset the world was ultimately stopped by the expedition team.

The consequences of the conflict nevertheless extended beyond her defeat. Arzen subsequently brought about a change through which the Authorities would no longer pass to new generations of Ascendants, fulfilling part of Amara's desire to free future generations from their continued influence.

The precise implications of this change, including its effects upon surviving Ascendants and the remaining alluvia distributed throughout Luciradis, remain matters of historical and arcanic investigation.

### Historical Interpretation

Amara's actions present a difficult contradiction in the historical record.

The woman remembered in Lyntoa devoted herself to protecting a child and encouraging him to discover a wider world. The Ascendant Slayer, later revealed to be that same individual, took the lives of others in pursuit of an ambition that would have denied countless inhabitants the opportunity to determine their own futures.

Her opposition to the influence of the Aurelants emerged from a desire to end suffering, yet the means she employed reproduced the coercion and violence she had come to condemn.

Nor can her relationship with Arzen be understood solely through either affection or manipulation. She granted him life, taught him, protected him, and encouraged his curiosity, while simultaneously concealing the circumstances of his existence and exploiting his extraordinary capabilities.

The survival of her divided soul further complicates these judgments, revealing aspects of her character that persisted through different individuals and manifestations long after her apparent death.

Amara's legacy therefore rests not upon a simple distinction between benevolent intentions and destructive actions, but upon the consequences of pursuing an ideal without accepting the freedom of others to choose a different path.
