## Background

### Early Life

Iris Riedel was born on Paragon's 8th, 10089 ASC, in [[Terystal]], an independent metropolitan city-state in eastern [[Luciradis#Nova Ciel|Nova Ciel]]. Born into a prosperous Akumari family associated with the [[Dreamweavers]], Iris spent much of her childhood under medical supervision following the diagnosis of a rare, progressive illness.

The condition gradually impaired her physical health, producing recurring episodes of exhaustion, weakness, and other complications that necessitated increasingly intensive treatment. Although her family possessed the financial resources and connections necessary to secure specialized care, available medical interventions proved incapable of reversing the disease's progression. As Iris approached adolescence, the prognosis became increasingly unfavorable, and she was eventually informed that her condition was terminal.

Her parents devoted considerable resources toward securing medical treatment, often imposing restrictions upon her activities out of concern for her deteriorating health. While these measures were intended to protect their daughter, their increasing emphasis upon medical supervision placed considerable strain upon their relationship. Iris gradually grew frustrated with her limited independence and the extent to which her illness dictated the circumstances of her everyday life.

These experiences contributed to her withdrawn disposition. Although naturally curious and academically inclined, Iris became increasingly reluctant to establish relationships beyond a small circle of trusted companions. Her familiarity with Dreamweaver traditions nevertheless provided an important connection to her Akumari heritage, particularly through practices associated with lucid dreaming and the exploration of shared dream experiences.

### Education at Gryffes

In 10107 ASC, Iris enrolled at the [[Terystal#Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine|Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine]], a prominent educational and research institution specializing in military and medical sciences.

Her admission was facilitated in part by her family's financial resources and institutional connections, which enabled her to pursue higher education despite the limitations imposed by her illness. Although Iris demonstrated genuine academic aptitude, the circumstances surrounding her enrollment and the accommodations afforded to her became sources of resentment among certain members of the student body.

Her reserved personality further complicated her relationships with her peers. While most regarded her with relative indifference, some openly questioned her qualifications or accused her of receiving preferential treatment. Iris generally avoided confronting these perceptions, preferring to concentrate upon her studies rather than expend her limited energy attempting to gain the approval of others.

Despite her difficulties, Iris maintained close friendships with three fellow students who provided an important source of companionship throughout her education. Their continued support allowed her to retain a degree of normalcy amid the demands of her treatment and the increasingly uncertain prospects of her recovery.

By the final year of her studies, Iris's condition had deteriorated considerably. Nevertheless, she remained determined to graduate, regarding the completion of her education as a personal achievement that would demonstrate her ability to pursue a life beyond the limitations imposed by her illness.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> The circumstances surrounding Iris's admission to Gryffes attracted considerable speculation among her fellow students, particularly concerning her family's influence and the accommodations made for her declining health.
> 
> It should be emphasized that such accommodations did not diminish the legitimacy of her academic achievements. Contemporary accounts indicate that Iris remained a capable and dedicated student despite the considerable difficulties she faced.
> 
> I find it regrettable that her circumstances were so frequently interpreted as evidence of privilege rather than necessity. It is perhaps easier to resent an advantage one can observe than to understand the suffering that made it necessary.

### A Fated Encounter

During her final year at Gryffes, Iris became acquainted with [[Arzen Lynos]], a traveler who had recently arrived in Terystal following his involvement in events elsewhere in Nova Ciel.

Although the two had previously encountered one another within the institute's dining facilities, their first meaningful interaction occurred within an unusual shared dream environment accessible through certain forms of dreamweaving.

The realm contained recognizable features of Terystal alongside remnants of its historical Akumari settlements, including structures and neighborhoods that no longer existed in the waking world. Its independently persistent nature distinguished it from ordinary lucid dreams, although the precise circumstances allowing both individuals to enter remained uncertain.

Initially unfamiliar with one another, Iris and Arzen gradually developed a friendship through their recurring encounters. Within the dream realm, Iris displayed a degree of openness and curiosity seldom apparent in her waking interactions. Temporarily removed from the physical limitations and social expectations that governed her everyday life, she found herself increasingly comfortable expressing thoughts and interests she ordinarily kept to herself.

Their acquaintance eventually extended into the waking world, where Arzen became more familiar with Iris's circumstances and the difficulties surrounding her illness.

Recognizing her desire for greater independence, Arzen began instructing Iris in practical combat techniques and the application of arcana. Her participation in physical exercises remained dependent upon her condition, with instruction within the shared dream realm providing an alternative during periods when conventional training proved impractical.

Although Iris initially struggled with the unfamiliar demands of combat training, she gradually developed greater confidence in her abilities. These experiences also encouraged her to become more assertive in everyday interactions, contributing to a noticeable change in her disposition and personal presentation.

Previously content to avoid attracting attention, Iris began taking greater interest in her appearance and social activities. Although she retained her naturally introverted personality, she became increasingly comfortable expressing herself beyond the small circle of companions with whom she had long associated.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Arzen's involvement in Iris's life occurred during a particularly difficult period for both individuals.
> 
> Following the incident in Cerelicia, Arzen had become considerably more withdrawn than those familiar with his earlier disposition might have expected. His subsequent decision to remain in Terystal and undertake an otherwise unremarkable occupation appears to have reflected a desire to distance himself from the conflicts that had occupied much of his life.
> 
> Iris, meanwhile, faced the prospect of an increasingly uncertain future as her illness progressed.
> 
> Although their circumstances differed considerably, I believe their friendship afforded both an opportunity to rediscover something they had begun to lose: the willingness to look toward the future rather than remain governed by the suffering of the past.

### Experimental Treatment at Wareylt Labs

As Iris approached the final stages of her education, her declining health increasingly limited the effectiveness of conventional medical treatment. Despite her family's continued efforts, available interventions could only delay the progression of her illness, offering little prospect of a permanent recovery.

During this period, Iris became involved with [[Terystal#Wareylt Labs|Wareylt Labs]], a prominent scientific research institution founded by [[Lily Wareylt]].

The laboratory had been investigating experimental applications of arcana and alluvia, including approaches with potential relevance to medical treatment. Iris's unusual physiological circumstances and alluvia profile attracted interest from researchers associated with these investigations.

An agreement was subsequently reached under which Iris would participate in experimental research in exchange for access to specialized treatment unavailable through conventional medical institutions.

The decision was made against the wishes of her parents, who regarded the proposed procedures as excessively dangerous. Iris nevertheless maintained that the opportunity represented one of the few remaining prospects for overcoming her condition.

Although the research carried recognized risks, neither Iris nor those responsible for the experiment anticipated the extraordinary consequences that would follow.

### Overgrowth Incident

In 10110 ASC, Iris became involved in the catastrophe subsequently designated the [[Overgrowth Incident]], an extraordinary arcanic emergency originating from experimental research conducted at [[Terystal#Wareylt Labs|Wareylt Labs]].

The incident resulted in the destabilization of the boundary between physical reality and an independently existing dream realm, allowing hostile entities to manifest within affected portions of Terystal. Among the locations threatened was the [[Terystal#Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine|Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine]], where Iris participated in efforts to protect fellow students and facilitate their evacuation.

During the emergency, Iris deliberately isolated herself within an affected section of the institute to prevent pursuing creatures from reaching a group of escaping students. Although her actions enabled their evacuation, she remained trapped as the situation deteriorated.

Arzen Lynos subsequently attempted to reach Iris, but the extraordinary circumstances surrounding her disappearance remained poorly understood by those responding to the emergency. She was eventually recovered following the resolution of the catastrophe, having survived circumstances that initially raised considerable concern for her safety.

Despite her efforts to protect others, Iris became the subject of considerable public speculation following the incident. Her participation in the experimental program attracted particular scrutiny, while existing prejudices among certain members of the Gryffes student body intensified. Her closest friends nevertheless continued supporting her throughout the aftermath.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Subsequent investigations established that the circumstances surrounding Iris's involvement in the incident were considerably more complex than contemporary accounts suggested.
> 
> Although her participation in the experimental research was voluntary, the events that followed cannot reasonably be attributed to her own intentions.
> 
> It is equally important to recognize her efforts to protect fellow students during the initial emergency. Whatever speculation arose afterward, those actions remain a testament to her courage under circumstances that would have overwhelmed many considerably more experienced individuals.

### Recovery and Later Life

Following the Overgrowth Incident, Iris remained under medical supervision while investigations continued into the circumstances of the experiment and the physiological changes observed during her recovery.

Subsequent research eventually provided an unprecedented opportunity to treat the underlying condition that had threatened her life since childhood. Through continued medical intervention, Iris ultimately recovered from the disease, bringing an end to the terminal prognosis that had governed much of her upbringing.

Her recovery marked the beginning of a considerably different period in her life. Having spent years anticipating an early death, Iris now faced the unfamiliar prospect of planning for a future without the limitations that had previously defined her expectations.

The experience also contributed to a gradual reconciliation with her parents, whose efforts to protect her had frequently conflicted with her desire for independence. Although the difficulties surrounding their relationship were not immediately resolved, the circumstances of her survival afforded them an opportunity to reconsider their treatment of one another.

Following her recovery, Iris resumed her interrupted studies at Gryffes and ultimately completed the academic requirements necessary for graduation. The achievement marked the fulfillment of a personal ambition she had pursued despite years of uncertainty surrounding her health.

Iris maintained her friendships with those who had supported her throughout her education and subsequently developed a close friendship with [[Rietta Aschlera]], Director of Research and Development at Wareylt Labs.

Having completed her education, Iris later participated in the expedition to [[Luciradis#Cicatri Ciel|Cicatri Ciel]], undertaking a journey that would bring her into contact with peoples and regions far removed from her native Terystal.

Her participation represented a considerable departure from the circumstances of her earlier life. Once confined by the limitations of a terminal illness, Iris now possessed the opportunity to pursue experiences that she had previously regarded as unattainable.

> [!commentary] Kallie Myrai, Head Archivist  
> Having spent much of her youth confronting the prospect of an early death, Iris has since demonstrated a remarkable willingness to embrace the opportunities afforded by her recovery.
> 
> Her eventual graduation from Gryffes was an achievement of considerable personal significance, particularly in light of the circumstances that had threatened to prevent her from completing her education.
> 
> I believe her subsequent participation in the expedition to Cicatri Ciel offers an equally meaningful indication of how greatly her circumstances have changed. For someone who once faced an uncertain future beyond the walls of a medical institution, the opportunity to explore the wider world represented a freedom she had long feared would never be hers.
