**Driardos Myrkals** was a former ruler of [[Luxor Citadel]] who held the title of **Arcis Princeps**, an office traditionally associated with the Citadel's highest governing authority. Historical accounts describe an accomplished practitioner of arcana whose political influence extended across successive generations, although the circumstances of his apparent longevity remained poorly understood.

Recognized for his intellectual ability, commanding presence, and proficiency in both martial and arcanic disciplines, Driardos governed through a combination of political authority and personal influence. His administration was characterized by an insistence upon obedience and a willingness to employ coercive measures against those who opposed him.

Driardos's apparent death following the decline of Luxor did not bring an end to accounts of his activities. He subsequently became associated with a succession of extraordinary incidents involving [[Arzen Lynos]], culminating in the [[Shattered Dreams Incident]] in [[Terystal]] during 10110 ASC.

Investigations following that catastrophe established that Driardos's history extended far beyond the founding of Luxor Citadel, revealing a connection to the original [[The Aurelants#The Seer|Aurelant of the Seer]] and the events surrounding the [[The Scarring|Scarring]].

## Background

### Early Life

Driardos Myrkals was born during the Era of Genesis, long before the emergence of the political institutions through which he would later exercise his authority.

Surviving accounts of his early life are fragmentary, although later testimony identifies him as an individual of unusual intelligence and considerable ambition. He possessed an aptitude for the systematic study of arcana, approaching its practice through observation, experimentation, and the careful application of established principles.

Driardos also demonstrated an interest in the conduct of others, particularly the decisions through which individuals exercised authority over their communities. He regarded knowledge as a means of obtaining influence, believing that a sufficiently informed individual could anticipate the actions of others and direct events toward a desired outcome.

These convictions would remain central to his character throughout his subsequent history.

### The Original Twelve

Driardos eventually joined the company of twelve mortals whose journey culminated in the defeat of **Genesis**, the being traditionally regarded as the creator of existence.

Although the members of this company shared an opposition to Genesis, their individual motivations were far from identical. Driardos's participation appears to have arisen from a profound resentment toward the existence of authority beyond his own understanding or influence.

He did not necessarily oppose the exercise of absolute power itself. Rather, he objected to the possibility that another being might wield such power over him.

Within the company, Driardos contributed his knowledge of arcana and his capacity for analyzing unfamiliar situations. His intellectual abilities earned him a measure of respect among his companions, several of whom came to rely upon his observations during their travels.

Among those companions was **[[Nero Carnegoa]]**, a formidable warrior whose abilities and judgment Driardos held in unusually high regard.

Despite their differing temperaments, the two developed a close association. Nero's practical understanding of others often complemented Driardos's analytical approach, and their disagreements were not initially sufficient to undermine their mutual respect.

Their relationship would eventually become one of the defining conflicts of the period following Genesis's defeat.

### Ascendance and the Seer's Authority

Following the defeat of Genesis, Driardos and his companions acquired the extraordinary powers subsequently known as the **Authorities**.

Driardos became the original bearer of the [[The Aurelants#The Seer|Seer's Authority]], gaining perceptive capabilities far beyond those attainable through ordinary arcana.

His newfound abilities permitted him to perceive events and possibilities beyond the limitations of conventional observation. Through foresight, he could anticipate developments that had yet to occur, examining outcomes whose causes remained invisible to those around him.

Initially, these capabilities reinforced his existing interests in knowledge and prediction.

Over time, however, Driardos increasingly regarded his ability to perceive future events as evidence that his judgment was superior to that of others.

Where he had once considered himself unusually capable, he now believed that he alone possessed the understanding necessary to direct the course of history.

The distinction between foreseeing an outcome and determining whether that outcome ought to occur gradually lost its importance to him.

### The Prophecy and the Fracturing of the Twelve

At some point following his acquisition of the Seer's Authority, Driardos witnessed a vision depicting the eventual downfall of the twelve.

The precise circumstances of this revelation remain uncertain, as do the events he observed. Surviving accounts nevertheless associate the vision with a pronounced deterioration in his relationship with the other Aurelants.

Driardos became increasingly determined to prevent the future he had witnessed. His efforts extended beyond ordinary preparations against potential threats, developing into attempts to influence the decisions and conduct of his former companions.

He came to believe that their independence represented an unacceptable source of uncertainty. Even those whose abilities he had once respected were now capable of making choices that might undermine the future he intended to secure.

Nero Carnegoa was among those who resisted this growing interference.

As the original bearer of the [[The Aurelants#The Paragon|Paragon's Authority]], Nero had undertaken the protection of communities threatened by the escalating conflict among the Aurelants. His responsibilities brought him into increasingly direct opposition with Driardos, whose attempts to impose his preferred outcomes threatened the independence of those under Nero's protection.

Their former friendship deteriorated as neither proved willing to abandon their convictions.

For Driardos, Nero's opposition represented more than a political disagreement. It was a rejection by someone he had once considered worthy of his confidence.

### Conflict with the Paragon

The relationship between Driardos and Nero ultimately fractured during the hostilities remembered as the Scarring.

Historical accounts describe an attack by the Seer upon a village within the territory defended by the Paragon. Although the full circumstances of the attack remain uncertain, its consequences are well documented.

Nero abandoned his defensive position and pursued retaliation against Driardos and those he held responsible.

The ensuing campaign inflicted devastation upon surrounding settlements and territories, undermining the protective responsibilities that had originally motivated Nero's involvement in the conflict.

Driardos interpreted these events as confirmation of his understanding of human behavior. Even someone he had once respected could, under sufficient pressure, be driven to abandon his own principles.

Yet Nero's retaliation did not produce the submission Driardos expected.

Their conflict consequently became a personal struggle extending beyond the disagreements that had first divided them.

Neither emerged from the confrontation unchanged.

### The Sundering

The Scarring eventually came to an end through the intervention of the [[The Aurelants#The Creator|Creator]], who enacted the Sundering and divided the Authorities of the other eleven Aurelants.

Driardos was deprived of the undivided Seer's Authority, its alluvia dispersed throughout the world alongside those of the other sundered Aurelants.

For a man who had come to regard himself as uniquely qualified to determine the world's future, the Sundering represented an intolerable defeat.

His efforts to prevent the downfall of the twelve had failed, and the powers upon which he had founded his ambitions were no longer his to command.

Yet the loss of his original Authority would not bring an end to his influence.

The means through which Driardos endured remained concealed for much of subsequent history.

### The Arcis Princeps of Luxor

During the centuries following the Sundering, Driardos became associated with the governance of [[Luxor Citadel]], eventually exercising authority under the title of **Arcis Princeps**.

The office occupied a position of exceptional influence within the Citadel's political structure, granting its holder extensive control over the institutions responsible for maintaining its government.

Contemporary accounts describe successive rulers whose identities and periods of authority appeared distinct. However, certain similarities in their conduct, political convictions, and treatment of opponents later attracted the attention of investigators.

These similarities were not understood during their respective reigns.

Driardos maintained his influence through a succession of individuals possessing exceptional affinities for the Seer's alluvium. The nature of this continuity remained unknown to most observers, allowing his rule to persist without the apparent survival of a single individual across generations.

Under his influence, Luxor served as a means through which Driardos could accumulate political and military strength while pursuing ambitions that had endured since the Scarring.

His authority nevertheless remained vulnerable to the actions of individuals unwilling to accept his rule.

### The Fall of Luxor and Arzen Lynos

Driardos eventually encountered renewed opposition in **[[Arzen Lynos]]**, whose involvement in the conflicts surrounding Luxor Citadel repeatedly frustrated his ambitions.

Arzen possessed the Paragon's alluvium and demonstrated a willingness to resist Driardos despite the consequences imposed upon himself and those close to him.

The resemblance to Nero Carnegoa was not lost upon Driardos.

Both individuals possessed exceptional resilience and a determination to protect others, traits Driardos regarded as simultaneously admirable and exploitable.

At first, Arzen's opposition appears to have held a measure of interest for him. Over successive confrontations, however, that interest developed into a deeply personal resentment.

Driardos sought not merely to defeat Arzen, but to demonstrate that his resistance was ultimately futile.

The conflict continued beyond the fall of Luxor Citadel, sustained by Driardos's ability to persist through successive bearers of the Seer's alluvium.

His determination to subjugate Arzen increasingly reflected the unresolved hostility he had once directed toward Nero, although the two conflicts arose under vastly different circumstances.

Driardos's final attempt to reassert his existence would take place millennia later, in Terystal.

## Appearance

Historical descriptions of Driardos portray a tall, slender man with a commanding posture and a carefully maintained appearance. Standing approximately **193 centimeters** in height, he possessed a presence that often drew attention even before he spoke.

His attire reflected the status associated with his office as Arcis Princeps. Rather than relying upon heavy armor or conspicuous military ornamentation, Driardos favored refined garments suited to ceremonial and administrative settings.

His manner of dress emphasized the authority of his position, while his restrained movements and deliberate expressions contributed to an impression of composure.

These descriptions must be distinguished from the physical appearances of the individuals through whom Driardos subsequently manifested. Although his consciousness persisted, his hosts did not necessarily share the features associated with his earlier identity.

## Personality

Driardos was an exceptionally intelligent and calculating individual whose outward composure frequently concealed an uncompromising desire for control.

He spoke with deliberate precision and seldom displayed anger openly, preferring to maintain an appearance of courtesy even toward those he regarded with contempt. His confidence arose not merely from his abilities, but from a conviction that his understanding of events placed him above the judgments of ordinary individuals.

Driardos could recognize ability in others and occasionally expressed genuine admiration for individuals who demonstrated exceptional intellect, determination, or skill. Such recognition, however, rarely extended to an acceptance of their independence.

He regarded disagreement as something to be overcome rather than a legitimate exercise of another person's judgment.

This attitude became especially pronounced following his acquisition of the Seer's Authority. His capacity to anticipate future possibilities fostered an increasingly rigid belief that knowledge entitled him to determine the course of events.

When circumstances contradicted his expectations, Driardos did not readily reconsider his assumptions. Instead, he sought to identify the individuals responsible and bring their actions under his control.

He possessed considerable patience, pursuing objectives across periods that exceeded ordinary human lifespans. His continued existence allowed him to cultivate influence over successive generations, often approaching immediate setbacks as inconveniences rather than decisive defeats.

Yet this patience was not without limits.

Driardos reacted particularly poorly to opposition from individuals whose abilities he respected. Such resistance challenged the belief that superior understanding should naturally compel obedience.

His relationships with Nero Carnegoa and, later, Arzen Lynos exemplified this contradiction.

Although capable of affection or companionship in earlier periods of his life, Driardos ultimately subordinated these relationships to his ambitions. He remained unwilling to accept that others might legitimately choose futures different from those he envisioned for them.

His cruelty was frequently expressed through calculation rather than uncontrolled violence. He could acknowledge another individual's suffering without regarding it as a reason to reconsider his actions.

For Driardos, the continued existence of his own will mattered more than the autonomy of those whose lives he sought to direct.

## Expertise

### Martial Combat

Driardos was a proficient swordsman whose preferred armament consisted of a slender longsword employed alongside an arcane staff.

His approach combined precise swordsmanship with the simultaneous application of arcana. Rather than treating martial combat and spellcasting as separate disciplines, he incorporated both into a coordinated fighting style.

The sword permitted him to engage opponents at close range, while the staff served as an instrument for concentrating and directing more elaborate manifestations.

Driardos favored measured movements and carefully timed attacks, seeking to control an engagement through positioning and the exploitation of an opponent's mistakes.

His fighting style reflected the same analytical disposition apparent in his political conduct. He preferred to establish an advantage before committing to a decisive action, relying upon preparation and anticipation rather than physical force alone.

### Arcana

Driardos possessed exceptional proficiency in arcana, supported by extensive knowledge accumulated across his unusually long existence.

His original association with the Seer's Authority granted him extraordinary perceptive capabilities, including the ability to observe possible future developments and identify information beyond the reach of ordinary senses.

Even following the Sundering, manifestations of his consciousness retained an unusual connection to the Seer's alluvium.

Driardos's abilities were not restricted to foresight alone. His recorded alluvia affinities also indicate considerable aptitude for applications associated with the Radiant, Navigator, and Reflection.

However, the extraordinary circumstances of his continued existence complicate attempts to distinguish abilities possessed by Driardos himself from those available through his successive hosts.

The full extent of his capabilities, particularly those associated with the preservation and imposition of consciousness, remained unknown until investigations following the Overgrowth Incident.

## Subsequent Investigations

_The following findings concern Driardos Myrkals's identity, his continued existence following the Sundering, and the circumstances surrounding his final defeat. Much of this information became available through investigations conducted after the Overgrowth Incident in Terystal._

### Identity of the Original Seer

Investigations established that Driardos Myrkals was not merely a ruler of Luxor Citadel or an unusually accomplished Ascendant.

He was identified as the surviving consciousness of the original **[[The Aurelants#The Seer|Aurelant of the Seer]]**, one of the twelve mortals responsible for Genesis's defeat.

This discovery provided an explanation for the extraordinary continuity observed across the history of Luxor's rulership.

The individuals through whom Driardos had exercised authority were not simply successors who shared his convictions. They had been subjected to the continued presence of his consciousness, which overwhelmed their own identities.

The original Seer's survival therefore represented an exceptional phenomenon distinct from ordinary Ascendance.

Although an Ascendant ordinarily acquires recognition through the concentration of a particular alluvium, such recognition does not imply the inheritance of a previous bearer's consciousness.

Driardos's ability to perpetuate himself through successive hosts was an extraordinary deviation from that process.

### Persistence Through Successive Ascendants

Driardos's continued existence depended upon his ability to assert his consciousness through individuals possessing the Seer's alluvium.

When a suitable host became vulnerable to his influence, Driardos could overwhelm the individual's consciousness and assume control of their physical existence.

The host's identity was consequently suppressed, permitting Driardos to act through a body that was not originally his own.

Through this process, he sustained his ambitions beyond the limitations of any single lifespan.

The phenomenon also enabled him to govern Luxor Citadel through successive identities without openly revealing the continuity of its ruler.

The precise mechanisms through which his consciousness survived the Sundering and subsequently manifested remain incompletely understood.

What is established, however, is that his continued existence came at the expense of the individuals through whom he acted.

The process was neither an ordinary transfer of political authority nor a recognized form of Ascendant succession. It represented the imposition of one individual's will upon another.

### The Shattered Dreams Incident

In 10110 ASC, Driardos's surviving consciousness became involved in the catastrophe subsequently designated the **[[Shattered Dreams Incident]]**, originating from experimental research conducted at [[Terystal#Wareylt Labs|Wareylt Labs]].

The incident destabilized the boundary between physical reality and an independently existing dream realm, allowing hostile entities to manifest within portions of Terystal.

Among those affected was **[[Iris Riedel]]**, a student of the [[Terystal#Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine|Gryffes Institute of War and Medicine]] whose connection to the Seer's alluvium made her vulnerable to Driardos's influence.

During the emergency, Iris participated in efforts to protect fellow students and assist their evacuation. She subsequently became isolated within an affected section of the institute as the situation deteriorated.

Driardos attempted to impose his consciousness upon Iris, exploiting the circumstances of the incident to secure another means of continuing his existence.

His manifestation brought him into renewed confrontation with Arzen Lynos.

Unlike their earlier encounters, this conflict would not end with another apparent defeat followed by Driardos's eventual return.

Through an extraordinary manifestation involving the remnants of the Aurelants' powers, Arzen employed a spiritual blade capable of destroying Driardos's persistent consciousness.

The destruction of this remnant brought Driardos's continued existence to an end, preventing him from reasserting control over subsequent bearers of the Seer's alluvium.

Iris survived the incident and eventually regained control over her own abilities, later becoming recognized as the last known Ascendant of the Seer.

### Historical Legacy

Driardos Myrkals's history spans the earliest recorded conflicts involving the Aurelants, the development and eventual fall of Luxor Citadel, and the events surrounding Terystal in the Era of Freedom.

His influence endured not because he had achieved the absolute control he desired, but because he repeatedly found ways to continue pursuing it.

The Seer's Authority granted Driardos the ability to perceive possible futures, yet his actions demonstrated a persistent unwillingness to accept the independence of those whose choices shaped those futures.

His relationships with Nero Carnegoa and Arzen Lynos illustrate this contradiction.

Both individuals possessed abilities Driardos respected, and both ultimately opposed his attempts to determine the lives of others. Their resistance became the focus of a resentment that persisted long after the circumstances of their respective conflicts had changed.

Driardos's final defeat therefore brought an end not only to his continued existence, but also to a struggle whose origins lay among the original twelve.
